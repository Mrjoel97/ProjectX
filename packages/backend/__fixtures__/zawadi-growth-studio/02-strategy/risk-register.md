# Risk register

Document status: CURRENT  
Reviewed: 2026-08-01

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early warning | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified lead flow stays inconsistent | High | High | Fewer than four qualified conversations in a month | Execute weekly outreach and partner cadence before adding channels |
| Founder overcapacity | Medium | High | More than 30 delivery hours for two consecutive weeks | Limit concurrent Sprints to two and use the standard delivery sequence |
| Client access delays | High | Medium | Analytics or website access missing by kickoff | Use access checklist and move kickoff rather than compress delivery |
| Late final payments | Medium | Medium | Handover invoice unpaid after seven days | Do not release editable implementation assets before agreed payment |
| Vendor quality issue | Low | Medium | Tracking change fails QA | Use scoped statement of work and founder acceptance checklist |
| Customer-data misuse | Low | High | Data requested outside agreed purpose | Minimize data access, document purpose, and delete exports after analysis |
