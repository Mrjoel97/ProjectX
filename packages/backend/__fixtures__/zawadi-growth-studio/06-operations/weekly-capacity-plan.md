# Weekly capacity plan

Document status: CURRENT  
Effective date: 2026-08-03  
Owner: Asha Mrema

Asha is the only person working in Zawadi Growth Studio. The normal workweek is planned at 40 hours.

| Work category | Weekly limit | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Client delivery | 30 hours | Maximum, not a target |
| Qualified lead generation and sales | 5 hours | Protected even during delivery weeks |
| Content and partnerships | 2 hours | Supports the lead constraint |
| Finance and administration | 2 hours | Cash, invoices, records |
| Review and improvement | 1 hour | Update templates and decision log |

Two Sprints may overlap only when their workshop-heavy weeks do not collide. If forecast client work
exceeds 30 hours, the response is to move a start date, narrow an extension, or decline work—not to
assume an employee exists.
