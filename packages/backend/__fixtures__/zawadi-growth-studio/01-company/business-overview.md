# Business overview — Zawadi Growth Studio

Document status: CURRENT  
Effective date: 2026-08-01  
Owner: Asha Mrema, Founder

## What the business is

Zawadi Growth Studio is a one-person consultancy helping independent East African hospitality
operators increase commission-free direct bookings. It is operated by founder Asha Mrema from Dar
es Salaam and serves owner-managed hotels, guesthouses, and tour operators in Tanzania and Kenya.

## Stage and structure

The business is in early revenue, is bootstrapped, and is productizing previously bespoke consulting
into a repeatable four-week service. Asha is the only person working in the business. There are no
employees, no co-founders, and no outside investors. Specialist work may be purchased from vendors
under fixed scopes; vendors are not staff.

## Offering

The flagship offer is the Direct Booking Sprint, a four-week engagement covering booking-funnel
diagnosis, offer and messaging refinement, analytics setup, and a 90-day action plan. Smaller audits
and optional month-to-month advisory support are also available.

## Customers and revenue

The primary customer is an owner-managed hospitality operator with an existing property or tour
operation, evidence of demand, and too much reliance on online travel agencies or referrals. Revenue
comes mainly from fixed project fees. Standard payment terms are 50% before work begins and 50% at
handover.

## Direction

By 2026-12-31, Zawadi aims to collect USD 8,000 in a month while protecting a maximum of 30 client
delivery hours per week. Its present binding constraint is not demand in the market; it is an
inconsistent flow of qualified leads, compounded by the founder's finite delivery capacity.
