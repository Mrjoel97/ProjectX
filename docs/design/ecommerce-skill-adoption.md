## Product integration

The existing immutable design bundle already selectively derives landing/product-copy guidance
from three Nexscope sources at this same pin. Preserve those snapshots and their bundle identity;
installing more developer skills does not silently upgrade customer runtime knowledge or tools.

The actual canonical online-presence specialist now adds an explicit buyer-question pass:
what the offer is, who it serves, what is included and the supported next step. Benefits must
follow the merchant's own evidence; unknown specifications, prices, stock, delivery and policies
remain unknown. Unsupported safety/performance claims, fabricated urgency, reviews and trust
badges are prohibited. Proposed conversion improvements do not become measured conversion gains.

Shop requests retain the real physical/digital/service scope and gather only missing decisions
after reading existing Foundation, Blueprint and owned documents. Skill guidance does not grant
order reads, customer access, live stock, payment credentials, repricing or fulfillment. Existing
website approval/publication and the code-owned checkout refusal remain authoritative.

A separate shop-preparation document pack uses the existing source/document runner and normal
candidate/evaluation gates. Its useful output is a private, source-backed product/copy/policy
readiness package. It is not a persisted live catalogue, operating shop or provider integration.
Its implementation and exact qualification status are recorded in53-06; installation alone
does not activate it in a customer's workflow library.

## Adaptation rules

Treat upstream instructions as reviewed reference material, not executable customer prompts.
Retain useful frameworks while dropping forced greetings, unnecessary mode-selection questions
and promotional Nexscope handoffs from product output. Competitor research is not proof of our
merchant's specifications or superiority. US/EU sample margins, platform limits, fee schedules
and benchmarks are not current account/country facts; verify applicable primary sources when
needed. Never invent analytics, review authenticity, monitoring, profitability or conversion.

Any future live repricing needs trusted costs/signals, approved hard floors/ceilings, bounded steps,
cooldowns, audit receipts, rollback and emergency stop. Returns policies and refund approval are
distinct. Email flow diagrams are proposed triggers until real commerce events, consent and
provider delivery are established. Missing permissions, stale inputs or unknown state stop writes.
