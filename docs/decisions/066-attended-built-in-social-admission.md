# ADR-066 — Admit the existing attended social capability on its own evidence

- **Status:** Implemented in source; current-source CI and release acceptance required.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Context:** The owner's repeated delivery instruction requires a usable engine instead of keeping built-in operations serialized behind unrelated qualification work.

## Decision

Replace the blanket empty managed-service admission with one code-owned attended slice: the social-marketing service may prepare reviewable drafts and read selected-account engagement reports. Admit only social.prepare and social.report, one exact active global cockpit instruction pin, existing native Executive grants and the same current tenant-owned Buffer/Zernio account scopes. Require the supported per-run budget, active control state and all existing readiness/configuration fences. No caller or environment variable supplies admitted operations.

This reuses capabilities already available to the ordinary Executive. Its acceptance requires current-source CI, real native readiness and the joined scripted public-start-to-draft/result path, followed by the relevant authenticated release checks. It has no technical dependency on qualifying the unrelated offer-architect candidate or approving each workflow pack. Their original qualification, activation and isolation requirements remain open and unchanged.

Respect the existing global registry's authority at read time. A unique already-active built-in cockpit row, including the supported bootstrap or previously activated rollback version, is runnable through the ordinary loader. Exact saved row/version/body identity and absence of an active tenant overlay remain mandatory. Do not demand a new evaluation merely to wrap that unchanged live capability. Candidate activation, instruction edits, tenant evidence/owner approval and the existing registry transition checks are unchanged; this creates no certificate or activation exemption.

Tenant overrides, extra instruction pins, workflow packs, business.review and verticals cannot enter through this slice. It grants no specialist dispatch, instruction activation, publication, new recipient authority or recurrence. Existing exact-content approval still owns Buffer scheduling. Native context, tools, provider admission, draft writes and terminal callbacks continue checking ownership, revision, scope and budget; unknown work/cost blocks replay.

## Consequences

The built-in attended engine can become usable through its own tested release rather than an unrelated bundle seal. This is capability admission, not a claim that Phases48–52, the authored-skill lifecycle, full social pilot or business improvement are complete. ADR063's recurring adapter stays disabled. No paid execution, registry mutation or deployment is authorized by this source decision.
