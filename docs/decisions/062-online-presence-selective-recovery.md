# ADR-062: Selectively recover websites into an attended online-presence service

Date: 2026-10-21
Status: accepted for isolated local implementation by owner instruction to proceed with multiple agents. Hosted activation and deployment remain separate release actions.

## Context

The owner wants customers to receive a landing page, website or shop as an agent-prepared package informed by their business Foundation and documents. Discovery found substantial earlier website and merchant work on unmerged PR36 at `5d7b9e5a8e0acaecbdc110123718798565421b5c`. That branch diverged from October main and its Phase48–50 directory meanings conflict with the current readiness/managed-service programme. Merging it wholesale would overwrite unrelated work and would incorrectly transfer old qualification to a different source set.

## Decision

Use the isolated `codex/online-presence-discovery-20261021` worktree and selective module recovery. Integrate the pure structured-document/render/recipe core and tenant-scoped project/version/public-form adapters with October main's lifecycle, registry, audit and export/deletion controls. Record this lane as Phase53 without renumbering older histories or claiming completion of current managed-service phases48–52.

Run the attended service through the existing bounded specialist runner and a versioned registry body. Bind its context, writes and private project receipt to a server-owned tenant/thread/plan. Use the exact frozen narrow tool tuple to distinguish the service from other specialists. The customer supplies unresolved business facts and reviews generated pages; customers do not author JSON, prompts, code, or workflow wiring. Private document use preserves warnings and does not authorize autonomous public disclosure.

Approval is a human action bound to an immutable version and hash. Publication, updates, unpublish and rollback stay on authenticated tenant UI/API controls, outside the service's model tools. Public delivery serves stored approved bytes, and later drafts cannot silently replace them. The only new scheduled work is fixed privacy retention for expired inquiry receipts.

Shop checkout stays unavailable until the merchant lifecycle is implemented and qualified. Existing invoice connectors and Pikar's subscription merchant account are separate authorities. Preserve recorded Stripe/PayPal, merchant-account, product, currency, hold and stock-race decisions; no guessed checkout, legal policy, consent or fulfillment is generated as if operational.

## Consequences and evidence

The useful website substrate can advance without importing the entire historical branch or opening a generalized unattended business-write engine. New compiler and runtime identities deliberately invalidate old recipe evidence. Local deterministic and scripted integration checks are evidence of source behavior only. Hosted recipe browser qualification, model acceptance and production release remain explicitly open in Phase53 and do not borrow the prior branch's verdicts.
