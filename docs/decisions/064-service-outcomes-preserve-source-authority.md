# ADR-064 — Service outcomes preserve source authority

- **Status:** Accepted for local implementation; hosted qualification remains separate.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Context:** The existing agent, goal, foundation and provider stores already own business intentions, confirmed publication history, execution and cost. A managed service needs a reliable connection between those records and historical reporting without turning completed work into a business-success claim.

## Decision

Reuse the tenant-owned goal reference and immutable run linkage. Explicit user goal binding fences prior configuration/control revisions; a new run copies that goal reference. User usefulness feedback is independent of operational status. Neither successful execution nor accepted usefulness writes goal achievement, changes business facts or activates instructions.

Capture bounded metric history only within the accepted Zernio report-completion transaction, after the current native connection, revision and lease checks. Retain the existing native publication window, account/platform/post/source scope, metric presence and upstream timestamps. Canonical identity excludes retrieval time and normalizes ordering. Deduplicate only repetition of the latest identical snapshot, so a genuine later return to an older value remains historical. Keep at most 30 observations across the tenant and page at most 20 through current source ownership/selection checks. Classify the content-plane history for existing tenant export and erasure; retention never rewrites audit or provider spend.

Compare metrics only within the same native post scope. Missing differs from zero. Retrieval and upstream observation remain separate clocks; zoneless, invalid, future or absent upstream times stay unknown. Conflicting values at the same upstream time do not prove movement. A source failure, withdrawal, changed generation or changed configuration invalidates confident use. Post-lifetime movement establishes neither campaign causality, Buffer publisher identity nor improvement of a business goal. Do not sum overlapping reach or engagement rates.

Preserve trusted run lineage and native channel platform on Buffer drafts. A matching Buffer/Zernio ID string is insufficient to connect their identities. The real outcome projection omits native publisher mapping until a trusted persisted receipt producer exists; synthetic positive inputs are not a producer or live evidence.

The cited brain view reuses existing profile documents and foundation revisions rather than creating another fact store. Agent source labels, document kinds and unchanged row creation times cannot confirm or refresh facts. Exact user-published snapshot/reference/hash evidence with present owned source references may retain its original confirmation time. That snapshot publication does not convert every agent claim into userProvided. Changed or withdrawn evidence removes the confirmation view. Code-owned next actions explain missing/stale/unknown evidence and user feedback; they do not create plans, start another cycle, rewrite goals or self-activate.

Cost remains in spendEvents and activity in agentSteps. Outcome views return their existing join references, never another stored bill. Audit writes contain only closed reference/revision/state feedback metadata, not goals, replies, report bodies or business prose.

## Consequences

Local native and pure tests can prove tenant isolation, trusted historical ingestion, clock/identity semantics, bounded retention and conservative explanations without buying a provider run. Current-source release, authenticated browser acceptance, the complete Business Ledger interface and repeated actual service cycles remain independent requirements. The reporting history and view are implemented; business improvement, universal ROI and publisher attribution remain unestablished.
