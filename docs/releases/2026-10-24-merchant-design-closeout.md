# Merchant runtime and design closeout

This isolated release adds three fictional examples rendered by the actual website compiler: Quiet Form (minimal website), Stillwater (glass landing page), and Soft Form (clay shop). Their real HTML headlines, copy and actions overlay the hero imagery. Six local generated illustrations have checked byte hashes and explicit provenance; they are never customer business evidence. See [the design review](../design/online-presence-v6-examples.md).

The online-presence agent can prepare a sourced private shop catalogue and operating package. Native exact-package approval writes actual product prices, stock, mappings, policy and bounded automation. Title-only revisions preserve sold stock. Commercial changes invalidate stale approvals. The specialist has no approval, publication, credential or payment execution tool.

Sandbox merchant adapters cover account onboarding/capability readback, authoritative carts, inventory holds, checkout attempts, signed payment reconciliation, unknown-outcome handling, native refunds and approved digital access/revocation. Stripe is the first required sandbox provider; PayPal remains separately gated. GBP is the requested currency; the supplied seller country is Tanzania. Currency does not establish provider eligibility.
