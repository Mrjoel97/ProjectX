# Phase 31 market research brief

Reviewed 2026-09-10. Scope: evidence for local Marketing-surface implementation, not a campaign or permission to publish. Product facts come from the repository; the marketing skill's older ADK/Supabase/ten-agent description does not describe this implementation.

The working audience is solo operators and small teams already using the private beta. This is the repository's audience definition, not a measured customer segment. The local task is to expose channel readiness, share stored artifacts through bounded links, and record externally captured leads through the existing Contacts store.

Audience pain hypotheses are repeat work, context scattered across tools, and uncertainty about what an agent may send. These remain hypotheses: no customer interviews, audience survey, or purchase attribution was collected in this task. Vendor wording describes their positioning, not independent demand evidence.

Budget, channel-account audience sizes, conversion baselines and acquisition targets are unknown. No paid-spend plan is warranted. Phase 31 raw GET counters cannot validate unique-user acquisition or conversion rates.
