# Proposed ADR-052 — Sweep-only recurrence D6 evidence wording

- **Status:** Proposed for exact-text owner review; not accepted, implemented or an eligibility verdict.
- **Proposed path if accepted:** `docs/decisions/052-sweep-only-recurrence-d6-evidence-reconciliation.md`.
- **Drafted:** 2026-09-25.
- **Authority sought:** A narrow successor to [ADR-050](../../../docs/decisions/050-recurrence-build-for-evidence.md)'s candidate D6 evidence phrase, conditional on the [ADR-051](../../../docs/decisions/051-sweep-only-pause-fencing-for-recurring-routines.md) sweep-only invariant. No earlier route preference or ADR-051 acceptance accepts this distinct text.

## Context and exact collision

[ADR-046 D6](../../../docs/decisions/046-a-standing-approval-reads-and-prepares-it-never-sends.md) says pause writes stopped state, cancels a pending scheduled function, and the callback re-reads status/version after claiming. ADR-051 conditionally replaces only the per-routine pending-function cancellation and callback-specific wording when a bounded shared sweep has **no per-routine callback, ID, self-arm chain or stored next-arm timestamp** and proves transactionally ordered pause fencing. ADR-050's *Evidence and exit* paragraph still literally demands “pause/cancel plus callback re-read” from its isolated candidate. A candidate with no callback cannot literally cancel or re-read one, so that sentence must not be marked satisfied by wordplay, a stub callback or a source scan.

The current six-file candidate has 25 passing synthetic tests and an independent design review, but it has no deployed sweep selector, real spend rails, real per-run approval integration, production reachability or live traces. It stores `nextDueUtcMs` and next-local fields, not the future zone-plus-last-occurrence production design. It has a test-only reconciliation entry point with no scheduled driver. These are limitations, not D6 evidence to be relabelled `live` or `pass`.

## Proposed decision

For a recurrence implementation **only while** ADR-051's no-pending-function invariant is independently proven on actual source/reachability, supersede solely ADR-050's candidate-evidence words “pause/cancel plus callback re-read” with this obligation:

> Prove immediate pause/revoke state-and-version fencing and a bounded shared sweep with no per-routine pending callback. A stale selector must re-read current status, version, due-ness and occurrence identity in its atomic claim. Reservation admission and each uniquely identified paid-step admission must re-read the current fence transactionally; one outstanding paid step and a single-use start claim prevent replay or a second step from calling twice. A reservation admitted before pause may settle afterward only on a reconciled/terminal path. A paid step admitted before pause may physically start or finish afterward, but landing re-reads the fence, cannot produce an actionable plan or successor admission, and releases the whole-run reservation on its terminal path. Every external-action admission separately requires per-run human approval and a current fence. Pause-first refuses new admissions; claim/admission-first work is bounded by these terminal rules. Test both commit orders and the adverse restart, retry, delayed sweep and DST paths.

The replacement is an **evidence specification**, not a declaration that the present candidate or future production runtime has met it. The required production proof remains ADR-051's full evidence list: actual bounded sweep selection and reachability, tenant isolation, claim races, both DST directions, real spend-rail idempotent reserve/refund/compensation, complete terminal and recovery liveness, per-run approval/action admission, and independent actual-source review. The candidate's synthetic results may support design review only. A missing sweep, pending callback, unproven rail effect, unclosed reservation or absent live trace keeps the D6 row missing.

If any per-routine pending scheduled function, stored callback ID, self-arm chain or stored next-arm timestamp is introduced, this exception does **not** apply. ADR-046's original cancellation plus callback re-read obligation, and ADR-050's literal candidate wording for that architecture, remain unresolved until a further reviewed decision and matching tests. A shared cron must never be canceled for one tenant's pause. An unrelated one-shot `dstProbe` evidence job is not a routine callback.

## Unchanged authority and machine boundary

This proposal does not supersede ADR-046 D1–D5 or D7–D9, D6's immediate pause/revoke and authoritative state/version boundary, ADR-050's `defer` → `build-for-evidence` → `enable-safe` ordering, six-file isolated candidate inventory, expiry on 2026-12-31, forbidden tenant/production/provider/paid/outbound behavior, three separate live-evidence requirements, or the separate owner release decision. It does not edit ADR-046, ADR-050, ADR-051, the historical 29 `defer` decision or any matrix row. No standing approval becomes authority to send. `enable-safe` still needs all twelve substantively reviewed rows and distinct collected real DST, seven-day OAuth-expiry/reconnect/no-burst and unattended provider-read traces.

**No stage-frontmatter change is proposed.** ADR-050's initial stage contract says its fields are *exactly* `stage`, `status`, `adrPath`, `adrSha256`, `reviewer`, `reviewedAt`, `expiresAt`, `environment`, `candidateFiles`, `tenantActivation`, `productionDeployment`, `providerCalls`, `paidCalls`, `externalWrites` and `externalSends`. The existing accepted stage still derives its build-for-evidence authority solely from ADR-050, with those exact fields and six exact paths. This ADR changes only what D6 evidence would have to demonstrate; it grants no new stage capability, so adding an `adr052` field would unnecessarily supersede ADR-050's closed contract. If this draft is accepted, the machine-checkable link is a **separate exact path/SHA-256/accepted-status ADR-052 identity predicate** in the historical defer checker, distinct from the ADR-050 and ADR-051 predicates. The stage validator and frontmatter remain byte-identical and continue to enforce ADR-050's original authority. A missing ADR-052 leaves the existing stage and historical defer valid; an altered, forged, extra or recurrence-matching renamed ADR must fail the defer filename scan. No predicate or stage pass changes D6 or `--eligibility` semantics.

## Required transition and negative controls

Before this proposal can become an accepted ADR, an independent reviewer must approve its exact draft bytes for owner presentation and record SHA-256. The owner must then explicitly accept those exact bytes and the no-stage-field-change decision; earlier acceptance of ADR-050/051, general implementation permission, silence or a reviewer's GO do not substitute. A later bounded transition may create the accepted ADR only with reviewed mechanical status/provenance/link changes and an exact checker exception. It must capture pre-edit hashes and byte-exact backups of the current checker/test/playbook; compare before each edit; test the actual CLI with the accepted path, altered bytes/status/hash, missing ADR, extra or recurrence-matching renamed ADR, ADR-050/051 tampering and unchanged stage-field refusal; and independently review final bytes. Only an exact accepted identity may be exempt from the existing recurrence-ADR filename scan. A renamed ADR with **no** recurrence-related filename token is outside that filename scan; this proposal does not claim otherwise.

The four modes must retain their distinct results on the current artifacts: `--matrix` 0, `--eligibility` 1 with its substantive findings, `--validate-decision` 0 for historical `defer`, and `--validate-stage` 0 for disabled candidate development. A failure during a partially applied transition stops the work. Recover only exact plan-owned checker/test/playbook bytes from verified backups and remove only the newly created ADR-052 after confirming its identity and no concurrent drift; leave the accepted ADR-050/051, stage, historical decision, candidate and DST probes untouched. Never weaken the checker to make a temporary partial state green.

## Remaining gaps and stop condition

Operational recurrence stays `defer`, with the manual rerun as the user path. No tenant activation, production deployment/migration, provider read or paid call from the candidate, external write/send, production sweep, real rail integration or live-evidence claim is authorized here. The accepted ADR-051 conditions, candidate limitations, automatic recovery liveness, actual revoke path, real per-run approval and all three live traces still require separate implementation and review. The four already-armed DST probe module and `arm`/`observe` export names stay stable through the last scheduled fire.

If the no-pending-function invariant, transaction order, rail compensation, approval boundary, exact owner verdict, checker negative controls or independent final review fails, leave D6 missing and operational `defer` in force. This proposed text must remain under planning until separately accepted; a planning draft is not a recurrence-ADR exception and is not product authority.
