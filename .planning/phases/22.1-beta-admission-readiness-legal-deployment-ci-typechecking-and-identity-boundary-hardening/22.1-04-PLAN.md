  <name>Task 2: The tenant-scoped export adapter</name>
  <files>packages/backend/convex/tenantExport.ts, packages/backend/convex/tenantExport.test.ts, packages/backend/convex/schema.ts</files>
  <behavior>
    - Export runs through the existing tenant-scoped wrapper from `lib/functions.ts`; it never takes a tenantId argument from the caller.
    - It walks `TENANT_TABLE_CLASSIFICATION`: `tenant_owned` rows are exported in full, `tenant_credential` rows are reduced to `{connected, updatedAt, scopeHalves}` with NO token material, `global` and `audit_immutable` tables are omitted with a stated reason in the export.
    - Every table read is paginated with a bounded page size and a total cap; no `.collect()`.
    - The export carries a header: schema version, generation timestamp, the tenant's own id, and the verbatim statement about the audit archive that matches privacy policy §9.
    - A two-tenant test seeds rows for A and B and proves B's rows never appear in A's export.
  </behavior>
  <action>

    Do NOT add a new table for export jobs unless the bounded synchronous path proves insufficient;
    if it does, say so and stop rather than inventing a job queue in this plan.

    `refreshToken` and `accessToken` strings appear NOWHERE in the serialized export, by scanning the
