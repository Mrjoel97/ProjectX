  append-only log. The audit module exposes ONLY insert functions — never patch, replace, or
  delete.' A tenant-deletion feature is the single most likely thing in this codebase to violate
  that rule by accident, because 'delete everything belonging to this tenant' is the obvious
  implementation and it is WRONG here. The privacy policy already resolves the tension and this plan
  must implement the policy's own resolution, not invent a new one: §9 says the audit archive holds
  'references, identifiers, hashes, and counts only — never the content of your messages, and no
  personal data', and concludes 'This is what allows an immutable log and your right to erasure to
  coexist: there is nothing in the archive to erase.' So deletion removes account data and content
  and DOES NOT TOUCH audit. If any audit row is ever found to hold personal data, that is a §4
  redaction defect to fix at the WRITE site — it is not a licence to delete audit rows.

  GOVN-03 REMAINS PARTLY OPEN AFTER THIS PLAN, and this plan must not claim otherwise. The Microsoft
  half of 'disconnection WITH revocation at the provider' is not closed here: disconnectMicrosoft
  returns revokedAtProvider:false and is documented in-source as NOT parity with disconnectGoogle.
  Plan 25-06 Task 2 owns the owner decision on that posture. Deletion must call the existing
  disconnects and REPORT what each one actually achieved, rather than implying both revoked."
