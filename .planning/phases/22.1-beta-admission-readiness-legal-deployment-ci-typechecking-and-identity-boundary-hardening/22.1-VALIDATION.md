## Anti-Vacuous Notes

Two defects in this phase were invisible to green suites, and both are recorded so the pattern is
recognised rather than rediscovered:

1. **Erasure was owner-gated and every fixture seeded `owner: true`**, which made the authorization
   clause unreachable. 6/6 green while the control returned `OWNER_REQUIRED` to every real signup.
   *A fixture that seeds the privileged state on every tenant makes the guard vacuous.*
2. **93 local `format` diagnostics were CRLF artefacts and five were real**, and a sampled
   generalisation dismissed all of them. *Classify per file (`git ls-files --eol`), never per sample —
   `w/crlf` is local noise, `w/lf` is a build breaker.*
