## Decisions Made

- Kept the export synchronous and stateless. Convex's one-`.paginate()` runtime limit is handled by one page per public query invocation rather than a new export-job table.
- Classified `users` as tenant-owned despite lacking `tenantId`; its access mode is exact `ctx.userId`, while every other tenant-owned/credential table shares `by_tenant`.
- Kept audit and refs-only dead-letter compliance records out of personal-data export, with the privacy-policy statement embedded in the file itself.
- Did not mark GOVN-03 complete. Export is now real; tenant deletion remains plan 22.1-05, and Microsoft provider-side revocation remains explicitly open.
