<objective>
Ship the user-facing onboarding: the forced-but-resumable first-run gate and the conversational
onboarding page (intake → extract → editable review card + persona confirm → commit). This is where
SC#1 (persona always confirmed) and ONBD-02's three intake modalities become visible product.

Purpose: match the "speak/type a goal" product identity by reusing the existing cockpit chat surface
rather than a bespoke wizard; keep the gate in the client shell to avoid the documented eternal-spinner.

Deliberate deviation (recorded for auditors — 11-RESEARCH Open Q1): onboarding reuses the cockpit's
conversational chat SURFACE (satisfying the locked "conversational, not a wizard" intent) but runs
persona/profile extraction through the separate UNGATED `business-profile` skill rather than the governed
`cockpit-agent` tool-loop (`runCockpitAgent`/`sendCockpitMessage`). This is intentional: teaching the
gated `cockpit-agent` body onboarding would publish an unactivatable EVAL_GATE candidate and endanger the
~25 golden fixtures (11-RESEARCH Pitfall 1). The split honors the "slot-filling conversational" decision's
intent while keeping every onboarding tweak out of the eval cycle.
Output: layout.tsx gate + dashboard/onboarding/page.tsx.
</objective>
