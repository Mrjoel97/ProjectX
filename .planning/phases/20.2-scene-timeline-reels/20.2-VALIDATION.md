## Escalated Automated Gap

### Animated image produces no decoded motion

- **Task:** `20.2-03`
- **Requirement:** MEDIA-01 / Wave 3 animated-image branch; Plan Risk 2 explicitly requires an
  automated rendered-frame invariant for zoompan motion.
- **Test added:** `packages/backend/convex/render/smoke_assemble.sh` now hashes decoded video frames
  from 8.5–10.5 seconds, wholly inside scene 2, and requires at least 75% unique samples.
- **Observed happy-path facts before the refusal:** 30.016-second output, `video,image,card,video`,
  durations `8,6,4,12`, offsets `0,8,14,18`, and 7.6 seconds of speech in the 6-second scene.
- **Exact deterministic probe:** `ffmpeg -v error -ss 8.5 -i <final.mp4> -t 2 -map 0:v:0 -an -f framemd5 -` followed by unique decoded-frame-hash counting.
- **Expected:** at least 36 unique hashes across 48 decoded frames.
- **Actual:** `MOTION_FRAMES=48 MOTION_UNIQUE=1`.
- **Classification:** implementation bug. The test was debugged twice: audio packets were first
  excluded explicitly, then a full-resolution video-only probe independently reproduced 24/24
  identical frames over one second. No production fix was attempted under auditor constraints.
