- **After a canvas derivation change:** run `mediaCanvas.test.ts`; rerun Playwright when making a
  fresh DOM/layout claim.

## Resolved Historical Automated Gap

### Animated image produces no decoded motion

- **Task:** `20.2-03`
- **Requirement:** MEDIA-01 / Wave 3 animated-image branch; Plan Risk 2 explicitly requires an
  automated rendered-frame invariant for zoompan motion.
- **Test added:** `packages/backend/convex/render/smoke_assemble.sh` now hashes decoded video frames
  from 8.5–10.5 seconds, wholly inside scene 2, and requires at least 75% unique samples.
- **Observed happy-path facts before the refusal:** 30.016-second output, `video,image,card,video`,
  durations `8,6,4,12`, offsets `0,8,14,18`, and 7.6 seconds of speech in the 6-second scene.
- **Exact deterministic probe:** `ffmpeg -v error -ss 8.5 -i <final.mp4> -t 2 -map 0:v:0 -an -f framemd5 -` followed by unique decoded-frame-hash counting.
- **Expected:** at least 36 unique hashes across 48 decoded frames.
- **Historical actual (2026-08-16):** `MOTION_FRAMES=48 MOTION_UNIQUE=1`. The test was debugged
  twice: audio packets were first excluded explicitly, then a full-resolution video-only probe
  independently reproduced 24/24 identical frames over one second.
- **Current actual (2026-09-25):** the full unsuppressed synthetic smoke exited 0 with a 30.016s
  reel, **48/48 unique decoded image frames**, valid sidecar/card checks, and the deliberately
  muted narration render refused as intended. The focused assembler suite passed 23/23, including
  shell/TypeScript byte identity. Holding the synthetic input and filters constant while reverting
  only `pzoom` to the historical `zoom` yielded 4/48 unique frames, below the 36/48 gate. The
  existing correction, not a new source edit, resolved the historical defect. See
  [the debug record](../../debug/wave3-animated-image-frozen.md) and `docs/playbooks/media.md`.
