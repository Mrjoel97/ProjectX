## Decisions Made

- **Vault-only uploaded video:** `Scene.asset` has only `{ source: "vault", docId }`; the render bridge checks the document's tenant and `video/*` type at consumption.
- **One governed sidecar shape:** old block sidecars return `legacy_sidecar`; no dual-shape tolerance was added.
- **One price source:** estimate and reserve paths share `SCENE_VISUAL_LINE` / `sceneVisualSpec`, preventing display and rail drift.
- **Mixed kinds are required by feasibility, not presentation:** 15 and 30 seconds cannot be composed from the provider's generated-video duration grid alone, while a 60-second all-video reel exceeds the job cap.
- **Ungated does not mean already active:** source and mirror are landed, but `seedSkills` must still run against the intended deployment and be read back before v2 activation can be claimed.
