# 26-13.1 — the image lane, and the comment that hid it

## What this was

Not a feature. A repair of a defect I shipped in 26-12, found by the owner at the 26-13 UAT with one
question: *"why is there no image tab? Because image is also Content."*

They were right, and the code said so all along.

## The defect, and why the comment is the real lesson

26-12's whitelist excluded `kind: "image"` under this justification:

> `image` / `video` → media intermediates, not shelf deliverables (the reel is the artifact).

**That is false.** `mediaComplete.saveImageToVault` is explicitly *"scoped to the STANDALONE IMAGE"*
(`plans.mediaMode === "image"`) and describes itself as `saveReelToVault`'s twin. A reel's scene
image shares the `mediaJobs.kind` token but **never becomes a vault document at all** —
`deleteIntermediates` removes its bytes at the render terminal, which is precisely why no doc is
written for one. So every `kind: "image"` *vault* row is a finished, standalone deliverable, and a
shelf of "everything Pikar has made" was missing one of the two things Pikar makes.

**The lesson is the comment, not the line.** A membership decision justified by a claim about
another module is only as true as that claim, and mine was written from the field name (`kind:
"image"` looked like a media internal) rather than from the write site (which says the opposite in
its first sentence). Both halves are now pinned by tests: a standalone image appears with bytes,
provenance and `promotion: not-applicable`; a bare `mediaJobs` row produces **no** shelf card.

## Search — narrows what is SHOWN, never what is LOOKED AT

The window is sliced to `limit` first and the title match runs over that page. Two consequences are
load-bearing:

1. **The cursor comes from the last row of the WINDOW, not the last row RETURNED.** Otherwise a page
   whose every row is filtered out reports `nextCursor: null` and strands the rest of the shelf
   behind a search term. Mutation-checked — swapping `window.at(-1)` for `items.at(-1)` reddens the
   named test.
2. **The result line carries the denominator:** *"3 of the 24 newest artifacts match — there are
   older ones on the next page."* A bare match count has no scale, and "nothing found" would be
   indistinguishable from "nothing found ON THIS PAGE" — the exact failure the bound contract exists
   to prevent.

`ponytail:` a substring match over the page, not a search index. The ceiling is real and named at
the call site: a term matching nothing in the first window reads as "no results" until you page.
Upgrade path is `withSearchIndex` on (tenantId, title) — a schema change and a second ranking to
reason about. Not taken.

## ONE ASSERTION WAS LOOSENED ON PURPOSE

`contentView.test.ts` asserted `api.vault.vaultDownloadUrl` appeared **nowhere** in the view. The
thumbnail needs it.

A flat ban would have been the easy assertion and the wrong one: **it forbids the feature rather
than the failure**, and the failure is a URL minted for a row nobody looked at. It now asserts the
call appears exactly once, inside `ImageThumb`, and that `ImageThumb` renders only on the image
lane — so a shelf of 24 documents still subscribes to nothing, and a shelf of 24 images subscribes
to exactly the 24 it is drawing.
