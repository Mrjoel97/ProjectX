## Decisions Made

- Kept the two honest endings distinct: no slides/worksheets is a broken archive and throws; parts
  that parsed but yielded no text return `""`.
- Kept cached chart values verbatim, including Excel date serials and full-precision percentages.
  Formatting them requires reading `<c:formatCode>` and remains a named upgrade.
- Did not attempt OCR for picture-only slides. The upgrade path is the hosted per-page OCR rail from
  15.2-06, with its real cost.
- Did not dereference `externalData`. It is attacker-controlled and, in the measured deck, points to
  the author's unavailable local filesystem.
