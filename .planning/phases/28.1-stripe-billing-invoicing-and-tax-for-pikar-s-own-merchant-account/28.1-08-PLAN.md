<objective>
Close the loop that would otherwise keep charging a card belonging to nobody, and seal the phase.

Purpose: BILL-06. `convex/tenantDelete.ts` says nothing about billing today — it reports only Google
and Microsoft disconnects. Deleting a tenant would leave a live subscription running.
Output: a third provider arm in `tenantDelete.ts`, a subscription-termination call, and the completed
playbook.

**Ordering is the whole task.** `deleteTenantDataPage` walks `deletableTables()` and
`billingCustomers` is `tenant_owned`, so the page loop deletes the row holding `stripeCustomerId`.
An arm added after the loop would have nothing to cancel. Google and Microsoft are already
revoke-first for exactly this reason — the billing arm is a third block of the same shape.
</objective>

running to the period boundary is exactly the failure BILL-06 names.
