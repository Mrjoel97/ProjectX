# Phase 28.1 Plan 07: The Invoice Rollup — Summary

## What this plan was for

Stripe has **no built-in recurrence for standalone invoices**, so the schedule is ours. That makes
the obvious implementation wrong in a way that is silent: **a cron pointing straight at an
`internalAction` that POSTs an invoice can drop a whole billing period.** Convex actions are
at-most-once and are **not** auto-retried; mutations are exactly-once; a cron run is *skipped* while
the previous one is still executing. No retry, no error surface, no invoice.

And the guard people reach for cannot hold either. **Stripe prunes idempotency keys after ~24h and
then treats a reuse as a brand-new request**, so a rollup retried a day later with the identical key
mints a *second* invoice.

## What landed

**Task 1 — `billingPeriods` and the claiming tick** (`50050d3`, `a46edd3`)

- `billingPeriods`: the durable claim row. `pending → claimed → posted | failed`, with `attempts`,
  a `failureCode` token, Stripe's invoice id and hosted url, and a bounded `charges` array whose
  `kind` is a CLOSED union (§4 — no free-text field exists for prose to reach a mailed document).
- `tick` is an `internalMutation`. The claim is read-then-patch under OCC (`spendLedger`'s law), so
  a concurrent second tick is re-run, finds the row `claimed`, and no-ops. `scheduler.runAfter`
  from inside the mutation is atomic with the claim.
- Three claim sources, and the third is what makes "never silently dropped" true: `pending`,
  `failed` (re-claimable), and `claimed` older than `CLAIM_STALE_MS` — a **severed scheduler
  chain**, which writes no terminal and throws nothing.
- `periodKeyFor(tenantId, atMs)` is the UTC month and **refuses a tenant id that is not ref-safe**:
  the derived idempotency key is an HTTP *header value*, and a CR/LF in it is header injection.
- The sixth cron, `billing-invoice-rollup` at 07:00 UTC, pointed at `.tick`. The cron-count pin in
  `reliabilitySweep.test.ts` moved 5 → 6.

**Task 2 — the bounded post and the settle** (`bde93c7`, `a46edd3`)

- `postInvoice` (the module's ONLY action) creates the document FIRST with
  `pending_invoice_items_behavior=exclude`, attaches each in-window line to *that* invoice by id
  with an explicit `period[start]/[end]`, then finalizes it.
- Every POST carries a distinct derived key; the invoice key is exactly
  `billing-invoice:{tenantId}:{periodKey}`. Key and body derive from the same inputs, asserted
  together (Stripe errors when a key is replayed with different parameters).
- `prepareInvoice` decides the whole document PURELY before a byte goes out: the cap, positive safe
  integers, ref-safe and unique refs, one currency (refused, never summed), and the half-open
  window `[periodStart, periodEnd)`.
- `settlePeriod` settles ONLY a `claimed` row — the second half of one-period-one-invoice.

**Task 3 — the hosted page and the proof no card data exists** (`ceb0f19`, `99ba44c`)

- `billing.invoices`: posted periods only, bounded at 50 with an explicit `truncated`, coverage
  reported separately (`known`-and-empty is a different payload from `unknown`-and-empty).
- The no-card-data scan now covers **`apps/web`** as well as the backend and the pure package — the
  half a browser actually renders — with a non-vacuity pin on the glob.
- A new **call-site** scan: every `stripePost(` in `convex/billing*.ts` must pass an
  `idempotencyKey`, asserting *which* modules it found. A unit test of the transport cannot tell
  you a caller forgot.
- `docs/playbooks/billing.md`: the flow diagram, 10 new invariants each naming its enforcing test,
  a `claimed`-period runbook with the `failureCode` table, and the gaps below.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **The invoice is created BEFORE its lines.** The plan specified `/v1/invoiceitems` first, then
   one `/v1/invoices`. That ordering **cannot achieve the bound the same plan demands**:
   `POST /v1/invoices` pulls ALL pending invoice items for a customer, so items-first relies on the
   sweep and inherits last month's failed leftovers. Invoice-first with
   `pending_invoice_items_behavior=exclude` + `invoice=<id>` on each line is the actual bound. It
   also forces an explicit `finalize`, which is required anyway: a draft invoice has no
   `hosted_invoice_url`, and that link is the whole tenant-facing deliverable.
2. **`billingPeriods` needed a `charges` array the plan's schema did not have.** "Accumulated
   invoice items rolled into one document" needs a source, and **this repo has none** — `spendEvents`
   is what Pikar SPENDS (wrong direction), and billing at cost would be an invented pricing model.
   The charges live on the claim row, written by a producer that does not exist yet. Said out loud
   in the schema, the playbook and below.
3. **The index is `by_tenant` on `["tenantId","periodKey"]`, not the plan's `by_tenant_period`.**
   `tenant_owned` puts the table on the erasure and export walks and both hard-code
   `.withIndex("by_tenant")` — the same trap 28.1-06 hit. One index serves both jobs.
   `by_status_dueAt` needed a written justification in `isolation.test.ts` (the claim scan is
   deployment-wide and has no tenant in hand; there is no tenant-facing caller).
4. **`MAX_PERIOD_ATTEMPTS` and `failureCode` are not in the plan.** Without a cap a permanently
   failing period retries daily forever; without a code, "we could not tell what paid this" and
   "Stripe refused" are indistinguishable in the row. Rule 2.
5. **`stripeHostedUrl` was lifted out of `billing.ts` into `billingApi.ts`** rather than copied.
   Rung 2. The second copy is always the one that forgets the scheme check.

## What this plan does NOT prove — read this before believing anything above

- **`BILL-04` IS NOT SATISFIED AND IS NOT CLAIMED.** `requirements-completed: []`. The requirement
  says invoices *are created programmatically on a schedule*. What exists is the mechanism, proven
  correct and bounded offline. **No invoice has ever been posted, and none ever will be until
  something writes a `billingPeriods` row.**
- **NOTHING PRODUCES A `billingPeriods` ROW.** There is no producer anywhere in this deployment.
  The cron will run daily at 07:00 UTC, find nothing due, and do nothing — for every tenant, every
  day. Every period in every test is seeded by hand. This is the same posture `reconcileEvent` had
  between 28.1-03 and 28.1-06, and it is the single biggest gap this plan leaves.
- **The plan gave no source for the accumulated charges and this repo has none.** `spendEvents` is
  what Pikar SPENDS on a tenant — the wrong direction — and turning cost into a bill would be an
  invented pricing model nobody decided. The product today is a flat-rate Stripe SUBSCRIPTION
  (28.1-04), which Stripe bills itself. What standalone invoices are *for* is a product question,
  not a code one, and it is still open.
- **Nothing in this repo has ever spoken to Stripe.** Every request above is asserted against a
  stubbed `fetch`. It is NOT evidence that Stripe accepts `pending_invoice_items_behavior=exclude`,
  that `POST /v1/invoices/{id}/finalize` returns `hosted_invoice_url`, that `amount_due` on a
  finalized invoice carries Stripe Tax, or that `period[start]/[end]` render the way we expect. All
  of that is read from Stripe's documentation.
- **Bank transfer on the hosted invoice page is COUNTRY-GATED and unanswerable here.**
  `BANK_TRANSFER_ENABLED` is `true` in config, but `HEAD_OFFICE_COUNTRY` is `null` and
  `CONFIG_CONFIRMED` is `false` — the business is not registered. The success criterion "payable by
  card **or bank transfer** through the hosted page" is therefore **half unproven**: we serve the
  link, and what that page offers is Stripe's answer, not ours.
- **The cron has never fired.** `crons.ts` is asserted by a source scan; no deployment has run this
  job, and the 07:00 UTC slot is untested against the other five.
- **`billing.invoices` has no caller in `apps/web`** — and a test asserts that. The invoice list is
  a query nobody calls, exactly like `unappliedFunds`.
- **The invoice list filters `posted` AFTER paging.** A tenant with more than 50 unposted periods
  could push posted ones off the page. Marked `ponytail:` with `by_tenant_status` as the upgrade
  path; unreachable while no producer exists.
- **A charge outside its period window is silently EXCLUDED, not counted.** That is what "bounded by
  the period" requires, but it means a producer that files a charge against the wrong period drops
  it from every bill without a record. Nothing counts the exclusions.
- **`prepareInvoice` refuses a period as a whole for a malformed charge**, so one bad row from a
  future producer blocks that tenant's entire month until an operator looks. Deliberate (a partial
  invoice is a wrong invoice), but it is a real operational edge with no alerting behind it.
- **The zero-card-data claim is now genuinely scanned** across `convex/billing*`,
  `packages/billing/src` and `apps/web/app/**/*.tsx` — but note it was *near-vacuous before this
  plan* (defect A above). The claim was true; the proof was not.
