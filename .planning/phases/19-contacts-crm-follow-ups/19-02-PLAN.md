    - `footerFor({ tenantId, recipient })` — reads `tenantProfiles.postalAddress`; returns `null`

    STORED (which in group mode is the comma-joined string), so the footer of a group send carries

      `raw = base64url(`${tenantId}|${recipientAsStored}`)`, `segment = `${raw}.${await hmacHex(raw, secret)}``
