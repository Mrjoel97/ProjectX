<domain>
## Phase Boundary

**Delivers:** the media-director specialist runs on its own model lane, chosen by a bake-off
through the production dispatch path and scored by the production parser — or, if no candidate
clears the pre-committed threshold, a recorded decision NOT to move.

**Also delivers, because the bake-off needed it and production was paying for it:** research
findings are reused for the same brief on the same tenant within 24 h, so a storyboard retry no
longer re-buys its research.

**Does NOT deliver:** any change to the skill body; any other pin; batch/composite/UGC/campaign
media; a model-override argument on the specialist loop.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **Lane, not repin of the default** — `MEDIA_MODEL`/`MEDIA_FALLBACK_MODEL` in `@pikar/cost`, a
  fourth arm in `runSpecialistTurn`'s skill-name lookup. Ships aliased to today's id.
- **Measure on the production path** — `dispatch:runMedia` on seeded eval tenants; the verdict is
  the plan row's `two`/`salvaged`/refusal code. No judge. `--repeat 2`.
- **Vary the candidate by the constant** — the runner reads `MEDIA_MODEL` off `cost.ts` (the
  `codeOwnedPackModel` idiom), refuses a mismatched `--candidate`, asserts each pass's `modelId`.
- **Candidates** — gpt-4o-mini (baseline), gpt-5.6-luna, claude-sonnet-5, gpt-5.6-sol. Rejections
  are recorded in the PRD with the reason each was rejected.
- **Decision rule is written before the run** — PRD L4. Clock truncation disqualifies; the winner
  needs ≥ 3 of 24 passes over the baseline or the pin stays.
- **Findings reuse** — keyed on (tenant, `contentHash(question)`, 24 h) via the existing
  `by_tenant_kind` index and a new optional `researchQuestionHash` on `vaultDocuments`.
- **Corpus** — 12 briefs about the seeded Northwind logistics tenant (so the vault has ground to
  find), spanning 15/30/60 s, figure-heavy and figure-free, thin and explicit, one illegal duration,
  one near the 500-char cap, one that asks for things the body forbids (a named song, a dissolve).
</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Pass 1 of the FIRST candidate pays the research for all 12 tenants; every later pass reuses it
  (L5). Report latency from pass 2 onward.
- Reports are JSON per candidate under `packages/backend/.tmp/storyboard-bakeoff/` (gitignored),
  plus a printed markdown table; ADR-032 carries the table.
</specifics>
