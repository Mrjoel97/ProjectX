# 33.2 — The storyboard bake-off, and the decision it forced

**Verdict: the pin moves to `or/openai/gpt-4.1-mini`.** 18 clean two-deck passes of 24 against the
baseline's 14, on the production path, on the 90 s media clock, every pass proven to have been
answered by the candidate. +4 clears L4's ≥ 3 threshold. The two premium candidates,
`claude-sonnet-5` and `gpt-5.6-sol`, are **disqualified under L4's clock rule**: both timed out
on the production path and rolled over to the fallback, now on the audited record.

Decided 2026-09-05 under the rule fixed in `33.2-PRD.md` L4 BEFORE any round ran. The pin lands in
`packages/cost/src/cost.ts` (`OR_MEDIA_MODEL`), the reasoning in ADR-032.

## 1. What was measured, and how it was proven

- Twelve briefs (`packages/backend/scripts/storyboard-briefs.json`), two passes each, one seeded
  Northwind eval tenant per brief, a fresh plan row and thread per pass.
- Driven through the PRODUCTION `dispatch:runMedia` by `scripts/run-storyboard-bakeoff.mjs`;
  the verdict is the production parser's, read off the plan row by `smoke:storyboardFactsForPlan`.
- The candidate is the code-owned `MEDIA_MODEL` (repointed in cost.ts, pushed by `convex dev`);
  the runner asserts `--candidate` against it AND against **the model that answered**, read from
  the pass's spend rows (`smoke:modelsForPlan`). A pass answered by any other model aborts the
  round (exit 2). Rounds 1-4 of 2026-09-04 lacked this assertion and are archived as contaminated
  (§4).
- Clock: `MEDIA_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS = 90 s` (PRD Amendment 1, L7). Research reused per brief (L5).

## 3. The arithmetic, by L4

1. **Primary — clean two-deck passes of 24.** gpt-4.1-mini 18 > gpt-4o-mini 14 > luna 11.
2. **Disqualifier — the clock.** sonnet-5 and sol each produced a pass the production clock cut
   off (`llm.fallback` audit rows, `errorName: "TimeoutError"`, `fromModel` the candidate). A
   model that cannot finish inside the production timeout is not a candidate whatever it writes;
   sol's three clean passes before its timeout are reported, not scored.
3. **Threshold — ≥ 3 over baseline.** 18 − 14 = **+4**. The pin moves.
4. **Tie-breaks** were not needed. For the record gpt-4.1-mini also has fewer salvages (3 vs 6)
   and fewer refusals (3 vs 4); it costs 2.6× the baseline per pass ($0.0081 vs $0.0031 — a
   storyboard is now under a cent).

**Variance caveat, recorded so nobody reads +4 as +4.0.** The baseline scored 16 on the 45 s
clock (contaminated round, own model on 24/24) and 14 on the 90 s clock: run-to-run spread on
this corpus is ≈ ±2. gpt-4.1-mini scored 18 under sonnet's name on the 45 s clock and 18 again
here. The margin is above the spread, not far above it; L4's threshold was set at 3 for exactly
this reason.

## 5. Why the premium candidates time out — measured, not guessed

Both fell over at the SAME point: the two-variation deck turn, 3-4k output tokens after a vault
search, against a 90 s clock.

- **sonnet-5 reasons by default on OpenRouter.** A direct probe with `max_tokens: 4500` and no
  reasoning setting spent 4 499 tokens on reasoning, returned **no text**, in 46.7 s. With
  `reasoning: { effort: "low" }` it wrote 5 459 tokens in 53.4 s (≈ 100 tok/s, $0.055). The
  lane sets no reasoning effort (`OX_ALPHA_SETTINGS` is the recorded, empty seam), so on this
  lane sonnet-5's default is to think past the clock.
- **sol** finished three decks at 64, 67 and 90 s and blew the wall on the fourth. It is simply
  slower than the clock at this output size, at $0.09 a deck — 11× gpt-4.1-mini.

**What it would take to measure them fairly is a LANE change, not a runner flag:** a per-model
reasoning-effort setting and/or the deck turn scheduled as its own action so the clock can grow
past the 600 s-action arithmetic. Both are out of this phase's scope (PRD "Out of scope"); the
first is the upgrade path ADR-032 names. Until then the rule stands and the measurement is
honest: on the lane as shipped, these models do not finish.

## 6. Corpus observations for the next corpus (not acted on here)

- `45-illegal-duration` asks for a target the parser refuses by design (`bad_target_duration`).
  Every faithful model scores 0/2 on it; luna scored 1/2 by coercing the duration. It is a
  refusal fixture inside a proposal corpus, so the effective ceiling is 22, not 24 — the ranking
  is unchanged because it costs every candidate the same.
- The skill body's 12-second generated cap is **not** parser-enforced: decks carrying 15 and 25
  generated seconds passed as `two`. A candidate that ignores the cap is not penalised.
- `narration_too_long` is the dominant failure for every model; it is a windows problem
  (per-cell narration vs scene seconds) before it is a model problem.
