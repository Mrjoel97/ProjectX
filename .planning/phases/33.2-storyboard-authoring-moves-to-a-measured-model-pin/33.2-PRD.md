> Every statement below is either a LOCKED OWNER DECISION or a fact VERIFIED against code, the
> local audit log, or a live API on 2026-09-04. Where a fact was verified, the verification is
> named so a later reader can re-run it rather than trust it.

---

## Why this phase exists — three drivers, in the order they bind

### D1. The storyboard is written by the VOLUME pin, and the volume pin says so itself

`runSpecialistTurn` picks a model pair from the skill NAME (`packages/backend/convex/llm.ts:5147-5160`):
research gets `[RESEARCH_MODEL, RESEARCH_FALLBACK_MODEL]`, a workflow pack gets
`[PACK_MODEL, PACK_FALLBACK_MODEL]`, and **everything else — `media-director` included — falls
through to `[DEFAULT_MODEL, CHEAP_MODEL]`**, i.e. `or/openai/gpt-4o-mini`.

The comment block that chose that default (`packages/cost/src/cost.ts:60-81`) is explicit about
what it is for: *"The default pin is a VOLUME pin — ingest, classification, routing — where extra
capability buys nothing … Quality goes on the pins whose output a human READS."* A storyboard is
read by the owner AND parsed against ~25 hard rules (`packages/contracts/skills/media-director.md`,
441 lines: closed visual set, exact second sums, a 12-second generated cap, per-cell narration
windows at 14 chars/s, two whole variations per turn, cited figures). It is the most
constraint-loaded single turn in the product, and it runs on the model chosen for classification.

33.1's PRD named this and deferred it: *"The media-director model pin … repinning is a separate
change with its own eval cost."* This is that change.

### D2. The measured failure rate is real, and the post-fix baseline is UNKNOWN

Audit of the local deployment on 2026-09-04 (Aug 10 → Sep 4): **55 storyboard attempts → 28
clean, 10 salvaged (one deck lost), 17 refused.** 17 of the 27 failures were `narration_too_long`,
and that class was a CODE bug in `widenNarrationWindow`'s donor search (fixed `32099f5`) — the
model was ~20% UNDER the total budget with the wrong per-scene split.

So the 51% clean rate is not gpt-4o-mini's number; it is gpt-4o-mini's number under a broken
repair pass. **A bake-off must re-measure the baseline on the fixed code, not reuse 28/55.** A
phase that repinned on the old number would be repinning on a bug it already fixed.

### D3. Every retry re-buys the research, and the bake-off would too

`runMedia` awaits `groundMediaBrief` before the deck turn: one research-specialist run
(web search, ~$0.21 measured — `run-eval-golden.mjs:175-181`) whose findings land in the vault
for `searchVault` to find. It runs on **every** `runMedia`, with no check for findings that
already exist — so "Try again" on a refused storyboard pays for identical research a second time,
and a 4-candidate bake-off over 12 briefs would spend ~$20 on research that is not the variable
under test.

Verified by reading: `groundMediaBrief` (`dispatch.ts:1340-1396`) has exactly three exits —
blank brief, `webSearchCalls === 0`, or persist — and none reads the vault first.
`persistFindings` (`research.ts:134`) stores `sourceThreadId`, `retrievedAt` and a title carrying
the (truncated) question, but no queryable key for the question itself.

## Locked decisions

### L1. `media-director` gets its OWN lane: `MEDIA_MODEL` / `MEDIA_FALLBACK_MODEL`

A fourth arm in the same skill-name lookup, two new constants in `@pikar/cost` beside the research
and pack pairs, for the reason `PACK_MODEL` already states: *a later change to one lane must not
silently move another.* `DEFAULT_MODEL` stays where it is — gpt-4.1 was tried there and REVERTED
because it broke ingest calibration and 13 tests; the volume pin is not the thing being changed.

The lane ships in plan 01 **aliased to the current id** (`MEDIA_MODEL = OR_DEFAULT_MODEL`), so
the code change and the model change are two commits and the first is behaviour-neutral. The
pin moves in plan 03, on the measurement, or does not move at all.

### L2. The winner is chosen by a bake-off THROUGH THE PRODUCTION PATH, scored by the production parser

Not a standalone script posting the skill body to OpenRouter. The runner drives
`dispatch:runMedia` on a seeded throwaway tenant, so every candidate meets the real prompt
assembly (`buildSpecialistPrompt` + `MEDIA_TASK_LINE`), the real `searchVault` grant, the real
step budget and the real `CALL_TIMEOUT_MS` clock — the pack lane recorded models that pass a
short probe and then time out at 113-164 s in production. The outcome is read off the plan row
`persistStoryboard` wrote (`two` / `salvaged` / refused + code), which IS the parser's verdict.
No judge model, no prose scoring.

**Why not the skills `EVAL_GATE`.** `media-director` is DELIBERATELY UNGATED
(`packages/contracts/src/skill.ts`, asserted in `skillBodies.test.ts`) — gating it deadlocks
seeding — so `run-eval-golden.mjs` cannot certify it and the gate's evidence rows are the wrong
instrument. The precedent is the pack runner's `--repeat` MEASUREMENT MODE: *measure, report per-case
stability, never write evidence.* A model choice is recorded in an ADR and a constant, not an
evidence row.

### L3. Candidates, from OpenRouter's live catalogue (probed 2026-09-04, `/api/v1/models`, all `tools: true`)

| Candidate | in $/M | out $/M | Why it is in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` | 0.20 | 1.20 | already the research AND pack pin; faithful 5/5 on repeats; cheapest "stronger" rung and already priced |

**Rejected without a run, each for a recorded reason:** the `google/gemini-3*-flash` family
(*"FABRICATES under generative pressure"* — cost.ts, a memo audit; a storyboard is generative
pressure); every `*-pro` tier ($30/$180 per M ⇒ ~$0.60 a storyboard, more than the reel's stills);
every $0 / stealth model (a free pin disables GRDL-03's downgrade and the ingest reservation —
cost.ts `OX_ALPHA_MODEL`).

### L4. The decision rule is fixed BEFORE the run

Twelve briefs, two passes per candidate (`--repeat 2`), same seeded tenant per brief.

- **Primary:** clean two-deck proposals out of 24 passes. Higher wins.
- **Tie-breaks, in order:** fewer salvages; fewer refusals; lower media-turn cost per pass.
- **Disqualifier:** any pass truncated by the CLOCK (`incompleteReason: "clock"`) — a model that
  cannot finish inside the production timeout is not a candidate whatever it writes.
- **Repin threshold:** the winner must beat the baseline by **at least 3 passes of 24**.
  Under that, the pin stays on gpt-4o-mini and this phase closes as "measured, not moved" — a
  repin on noise is the thing this rule exists to prevent.
- **Reported, never scored:** generated seconds per deck, kind mix, `unverified` counts,
  parser adjustments, latency on pass 2 (pass 1 of the first candidate carries the research).

### L5. Research findings are reused for the same brief on the same tenant within 24 h

`groundMediaBrief` first asks the vault whether a `web_research` document for this exact brief
(hash of the question text) landed on this tenant in the last 24 hours; if so it returns without
a turn. `persistFindings` stores that hash on the document (`researchQuestionHash`, optional →
no migration). This is D3's fix for production (a retry stops re-buying research) and what makes
the bake-off's research a one-time cost.

### L6. The credit goes where it always did — nowhere new

The bake-off writes nothing to production, nothing to `skills.evidence`, no audit row beyond what
`runMedia` already writes on its throwaway tenants. Model output is dumped to a local `.tmp/`
file only (gitignored), the pack runner's `--dump` rule.

## Scope

### In scope

1. The `MEDIA_MODEL` / `MEDIA_FALLBACK_MODEL` lane, priced, asserted, aliased (plan 01).
2. Research-findings reuse keyed on (tenant, question hash, 24 h) (plan 01).
3. `run-storyboard-bakeoff.mjs`, a 12-brief corpus, and a §4-clean facts query that reads the
   parser's verdict off the plan row (plan 02).
4. The bake-off itself, the pin (or the recorded decision not to move it), ADR-032, playbooks,
   and one owner-run reel on the chosen pin (plan 03).

### Out of scope — deliberately

- **The skill body.** Not one word of `media-director.md` changes here. A body edit and a model
  change in the same phase cannot be attributed (the skill-body-edits-shift-tool-args memory).
- **The research pin, the pack pin, the default pin.** L1.
- **Batch / composite / UGC / campaign media.** Parked by the owner until a reel completes; one
  has now completed, and they still wait for this phase.
- **A `model` argument on `runSpecialistTurn`.** The comment at the lookup refuses it as "a second
  mechanism for a decision with exactly one owner". The runner varies the candidate by the
  code-owned constant and ASSERTS the returned `modelId` matches; each candidate is one edit,
  one `convex dev` push, one invocation.

## Constraints that bind every plan here

- **CLAUDE.md §4** — the facts query returns counts and codes. Never a narration line, never a
  prompt, never a document title.
- **CLAUDE.md §5** — the briefs live OUTSIDE `convex/` (`scripts/storyboard-briefs.json`); they
  are user turns, not agent prompts, and the 200-char inline scan is blind to intent.
- **Per-tenant rails** — `DAILY_BUDGET_CENTS = 500`. One brief tenant spends ≈ $0.21 research +
  ≤ 8 media passes ≤ $0.75; the rail is not reached, and the runner carries its own cost cap.
- **No production.** Every call is `convex run` against the local dev deployment; `smokeRun.mjs`
  cannot target prod without an explicit env opt-in, which is never set here.
- **Playbooks** — `cost.ts` is `guardrails.md`; `llm.ts`/`dispatch.ts`/`research.ts` are
  `cockpit.md`; `smoke.ts` is `agent-runtime.md`; the runner and corpus register under `media.md`.

## Amendment 1 — 2026-09-04, after rounds 1-3 ran

**What the runner asserted was the PIN, not the model that answered.** `runMedia.modelId` is the
pair the lookup chose; an eligible primary failure rolls over to `MEDIA_FALLBACK_MODEL`
(`gpt-4.1-mini`) and the run still succeeds, with NOTHING in any plane saying so. Reconstructed from
`spendEvents` via the new `smoke:modelsForPlan`:

| Round | Answered by the candidate | Answered by the fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gpt-4o-mini | 24 / 24 | 0 |
| gpt-5.6-luna | 11 / 24 | 13 |
| claude-sonnet-5 | 0 / 24 | 24 |

Round 4 (gpt-5.6-sol) aborted on its first pass once the runner asserted the executed model.

**The cause is the clock, not the models.** Both refused candidates answer a direct probe; what
they cannot do is finish a two-variation storyboard inside `CALL_TIMEOUT_MS = 45 s`, the cockpit
chat-turn clock the media lane inherited. luna's finished passes averaged 45 s. The timeout is
`isFallbackEligible`, so the fallback wrote the deck — and has been doing so in production
whenever the primary ran long.

**Three changes, all in the lane:**
- L7. `callTimeoutMsFor(media-director) = 90 s` (`MEDIA_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS`). Not 180: `runMedia`
  awaits research (up to 2 × 180 s) and this turn (2 × 90 s) in ONE action under a 600 s ceiling.
- L8. `runAgentLoop` audits `llm.fallback` (from/to model ids, error NAME, stage `agent-loop`)
  before it retries on the fallback. The silence was the defect.
- L9. The runner asserts the EXECUTED model per pass from spend rows and aborts otherwise (B5 is
  restated: "fails a pass whose spend rows name a different model, or none").

**The decision rule (L4) is unchanged**, and every round is re-run under it on the 90 s clock —
the baseline included, because a longer clock changes what gpt-4o-mini's slow passes do too.
`gpt-4.1-mini` joins the candidates: it scored 18/24 on its own under sonnet's name, it is
already priced, already the fallback, and 2.7× the baseline's price rather than 13×.

**Blocked on funding.** OpenRouter balance at amendment time: $1.00 of $13.00. A full re-run of
five rounds at real prices is ≈ $4 (sonnet-5 and sol ≈ $1.7 each). The owner tops up; nothing
here can.
