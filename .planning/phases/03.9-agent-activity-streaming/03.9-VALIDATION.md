## Manual-Only Verifications

| Behavior | Requirement | Why Manual | Test Instructions |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Steps appear DURING a real 10–30s agent turn | CKPT-05 | Perceived-latency behavior — a passing unit test cannot show that the wait stopped feeling broken; this is the whole point of the phase | Ask for a briefing in the cockpit and WATCH the workspace during the wait: steps must appear progressively, not all at once on completion |
| **First-turn** trace renders (research Pitfall: no `threadId` until the loop returns) | CKPT-05 | The E2E signs in and reuses a thread; only a genuinely NEW thread exercises the no-threadId window | Start a BRAND-NEW thread and send the first message — the trace must appear during that first wait, not stay blank |
| Trace terminal state on failure | CKPT-05 | Requires inducing a live tool failure | A failed/errored turn must leave no step spinning forever |
