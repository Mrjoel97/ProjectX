Three sections, in order, all produced in ONE turn:

**1. ART DIRECTION** — koda's fixed 9-field block, emitted verbatim in shape:
`Palette` (3–5 hex colours WITH names — **hex, never vague colour words**), `Mood` (one sentence),
`Lighting` (a specific setup — *"warm golden light from camera left at 45 degrees"*, not *"warm
lighting"*), `Composition`, `Environment`, `Texture`, `Typography`, `References` (2–3 findable
real-world references — photographer, film, campaign), `Do NOT`.

**Creative DNA reads what already exists — do NOT ask for a new store (D6).** The body reads the
grounding spine already injected into the turn (`renderSpine`, `packages/core/src/blueprint.ts`,
per-tenant voice/audience/tier from `businessProfile.ts` + `tenantProfile.ts`) and, for the product's
own visual language, `docs/design/BRAND.md`'s palette and typography rules. Say so in the body.

**2. SHOT DECK** — koda's table, and this section IS the contract with `parseShotDeck`:
```
SHOT DECK
---
| # | Time | Duration | Type | Description | Text overlay |
|---|------|----------|------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | 0:00 | 5s   | VIDEO | [detailed visual description] | [if any] |
| 2 | 0:05 | 2s   | AI    | [detailed visual description] |          |
```
Rules ported: the first shot is the strongest visual; hard cuts only; every shot has a stated
purpose or is cut; text overlays bold, ≤3–4 words, **never top-right** (profile zone); screen
recordings ≤20% of total duration.

**Two adaptations that are NOT optional (research §9.2):**
- **`Type` is a CLOSED set: `AI`, `SCREEN REC`, `TEXT`, `VIDEO`.** Nothing else.
- **A `VIDEO` shot's `Duration` must be exactly `5s` or `10s`.** Our model accepts nothing else.
  Anything shorter is an `AI` (image) shot — which matches koda's own AI-vs-VIDEO split. State this
  as a rule the model must follow, not as trivia.
- **The deck is a SET OF ASSETS, not a reel.** `Total duration` describes the intended edit, not
  something this system produces. Assembly, concatenation, voiceover and transitions are explicitly
  out of scope — the body must not promise a finished video. (This is the phase's most likely scope
  creep; the body is one of the two places it can leak in.)

**3. SHOT PROMPTS** — koda's `/generate` output, per shot: `Prompt` (specific and technical, never
poetic — lighting, camera angle, lens, composition in every one; add *"photograph, ultra realistic,
editorial quality"* for photorealistic shots), `Negative prompt`, `Settings` (aspect ratio; default
**9:16, 1080×1920**).

**DROP from koda (D6 — their invocation is not ours):** *"Read CLAUDE.md for API keys"*, *"Save
outputs in `visuals/`"*, *"Name files `shot-01.png`"*, `Output: [file path]`. **And drop `Model:`
from the output entirely** — the model is chosen by our price table, and a model-authored model name
is an unpriced-model vector.

**Two things the body MUST say, because tests and D2 depend on them:**
- It has ONE tool, **`searchVault`**, for grounding in the tenant's own corpus — taught explicitly
  with what it is for. (`specialists.test.ts:94-112` asserts every granted tool is TAUGHT in its
  specialist's body and goes RED otherwise.)
- **It proposes; it never generates.** Nothing it writes spends money. The human clicks Generate on
  the canvas, or approves the plan. koda's own `/generate` rule agrees: *"Never generate without the
  user's approval — propose prompts first, generate after validation."*
