<objective>
The voiceover stage: one TTS take per block, through the machinery that already exists.

Purpose: D8 makes narration a stage, and delta §1 makes it a **priced line item on the same provider**.
`fal-ai/inworld-tts` bills **$0.01 per 1000 submitted characters** — a per-INPUT unit, which is the
only kind of unit a job reserved before it exists can be priced in. A 6-block 60-second voiceover is
~1,200 characters and **$0.012**: **0.4% of a $3.05 job.** The cap is bounded by the clips, and the
clips were already the locked constraint.

Two properties of the chosen endpoint are load-bearing rather than incidental:
1. **There is NO `speed` / `rate` parameter.** D8's *"no time-stretch, ever"* is therefore enforced by
   the PROVIDER, not by our discipline. `fal-ai/kokoro/*` has `speed: 0.1–5.0` and is a live foot-gun:
   a future contributor "fixing" an overrunning line by nudging `speed` to 1.15 would violate D8
   silently and no test would catch it (delta pitfall 15). That outranks any difference in voice
   character.
2. **The response carries no duration and no character count.** So `actual == estimate` by
   construction — plan 20-06's `EXACT_SPEND_KINDS` already covers it, and this plan spends its budget
   on the two switch arms rather than on a reconciliation path with nothing to reconcile.

Output: two switch arms, one narration read, and their offline assertions.
</objective>
