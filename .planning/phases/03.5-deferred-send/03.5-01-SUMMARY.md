## Decisions Made
- **Bare AM vs PM disambiguation:** the plan's behavior spec gave "4pm" → resolved but "4am" → ambiguous. Reconciled with a coherent, documented rule: a bare PM time reads as "later today, else tomorrow" (the low-risk interpretation people expect); a bare AM time (whose today-occurrence has usually passed, so resolving to tomorrow would be a guess) or a meridiem-less bare hour with no day → ambiguous → re-ask. A day anchor (today/tomorrow/weekday) disambiguates any am/pm time. This honors locked bound 3 ("never guess a day") while satisfying both plan examples.
- **DST handling:** `zonedWallClockToEpoch` does one offset-refinement pass — sufficient for minute-granularity slice-1 needs.
