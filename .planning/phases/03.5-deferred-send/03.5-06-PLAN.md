time. A resolved time beyond a tunable horizon becomes a new `tooFar` parse outcome that re-asks (never
a silent clamp), the picker caps its `max` at the same horizon, and the horizon is a calibration knob
with the ceiling + upgrade path named in a ponytail comment.

mitigation" item; slice 1 shipped with "No far-future cap"). The structural risk is Gmail Testing-
mode's 7-day refresh-token expiry (design/scheduled-send.md) — a schedule past it finds a dead token.
The check lives in the PURE parser (Plan 01's discipline) so it is deterministic + offline-SMOKE-able.
Output: the horizon constant + tooFar variant + the setSendTime handling + the native picker cap.
</objective>
