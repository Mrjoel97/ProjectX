## Gaps

### Gap 1 — The briefing is a receipt, not a report
- **status:** failed
- **Reported (verbatim):** "It's still looking overwhelming. It's not looking smart. It looks
  like the agent just took the list from the inbox and then put it up in the workspace. How
  about we make it an intelligent report? … categorized by maybe type, maybe business …
  something that is going to be informative to someone knowing about the brief of their inbox,
  not just the receipt of the emails they missed to read."
- **Diagnosis (confirmed in code, not inferred):** the digest ALREADY classifies every message
  into `action | fyi | newsletter | other` (`digestSchema`, `llm.ts:1781`) and persists it on
  `briefings.items[].category` — and `cards.tsx` renders it **zero times**. We pay a model to
  triage the inbox and then display the result in arrival order. The intelligence is computed
  and discarded.
- **Root cause is a PLANNING miss, not an execution miss:** CKPT-04 SC-1 literally specifies
  "each item shows timestamp, sender, and a one-line gist" — a list. The executors built exactly
  what was specified. The requirement was under-ambitious.
- **What "intelligent" means here (scope for the gap plan):**
  1. A **lede** — one synthesized sentence ("24 messages, 3 need you; nothing urgent — mostly
     billing notifications and two recruiting threads"). Requires CROSS-message synthesis; the
     current digest reasons per-message in isolation and never asks "what is the story of this
     inbox?"
  2. **Action-first ordering** — needs-you / awaiting-reply above FYI, not chronological.
  3. **Collapse the noise** — "12 automated notifications" instead of 12 rows. Expected to remove
     most of the "overwhelming" feeling on its own.
  4. **Surface `category`** — the axis already exists and is free.
- **Locked constraint — do NOT silently drop:** CKPT-04 SC-1 mandates today/yesterday/this-week
  grouping computed in pure tested code. This gap RESHAPES the axis (category/action primary,
  time secondary); it does not delete time grouping. Changing that requires a requirement change
  through the roadmap, not a quiet edit in a card.
- **Invariant constraint:** any new synthesis call stays TOOLLESS (agent-runtime invariant 10).
  Synthesizing over already-derived digests is safe; do not hand raw bodies to a tool-bearing loop.
