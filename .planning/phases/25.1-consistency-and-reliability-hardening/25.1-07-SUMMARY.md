## Finding: D4 is real, recurring, and currently invisible in production

Ten plans sit in `status: "collecting"` in production, aged from ~17 hours to ~5 days
(2026-08-16 → 2026-08-20). Every one is past the watchdog's `COLLECTING_STALL_MS` (30 min).

`collecting` means a specialist dispatch was scheduled and never landed. `PlanCard` renders only at
`proposed`, so **the owner saw nothing for any of the ten** — no error, no retry, no trace. This is
the audit's D4 prediction, confirmed in the wild at a rate of roughly two per day.

**Operational consequence of arming:** the sweep's first armed pass will terminalize all ten and
emit up to ten `watchdog.stalled` notifications at once (batch size 100, so one pass). That burst is
expected and correct — it is five days of silent failure becoming visible — but it should not be
mistaken for the watchdog malfunctioning.
