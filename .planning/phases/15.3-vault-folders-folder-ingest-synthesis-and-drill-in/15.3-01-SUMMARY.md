## Decisions Made

- **`by_tenant_driveFileId` shipped in wave 1 although only wave 9 uses it.** It is dead index
  surface for eight plans. Taken deliberately: the objective of this plan is that no later wave
  edits `schema.ts`, and one unused index costs nothing (Convex builds indexes automatically; the
  migration-helper skill lists index changes under *When Not to Use*).
- **`docType`'s twelve literals were copied verbatim from plan 08's `DOC_TYPES`.** Plan 08 owns the
  two-direction compile bridge that turns drift between the schema union and the TS union into a
  typecheck failure; until it lands, the two lists are kept identical by hand and by the comment.
- **`unclassified` vs ABSENT is left ambiguous on purpose**, with the distinction stated in the
  comment (absent ⇒ never classified; `"unclassified"` ⇒ classified and unplaceable) rather than
  resolved by a new field. Consumers decide; the grid is expected to fall back to the filename for
  both.
- **Requirements left `Pending`.** All ten VALT ids are in this plan's `requirements` frontmatter,
  but schema alone satisfies none of them — every one names user-observable behaviour owned by
  waves 2-9. `gsd-tools requirements mark-complete` was deliberately NOT run, following the
  17.1-01 precedent where an early flip was reverted as a false signal.
