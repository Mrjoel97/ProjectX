**An inline `clay-card` panel replacing the Dropzone body once files are picked — NOT a modal.** On
record: only two `aria-modal` blocks exist in the app, neither is shared, `PreviewModal` has Esc +
scroll-lock but **no focus trap**, and `DisconnectGoogle.tsx:25-27` documents the deliberate absence
of a dialog pattern. An inline step needs no focus trap, no scroll lock, no portal — and does not
inherit that a11y gap (BRAND §6).

Four `Kpi` tiles in the `BlueprintPanel.tsx:339` shape (bold, `fontVariantNumeric:"tabular-nums"`,
uppercase caption): **N FILES · TOTAL SIZE · EST. COST · READY IN**, plus the skipped list and an
explicit **Start**. Import `fmtSize` from `DocGrid.tsx:42`; money is `(cents/100).toFixed(2)` inline
(no helper exists, and `fmtSize` is the precedent for a one-liner beside its consumer).

**READY IN must be honest.** It is two terms: transfer (`totalBytes / upstream`) **plus** processing
(`ceil(docCount / parallelism) × per-doc time`), and processing dominates — 400 documents at
concurrency 6 is tens of minutes. Show the honest figure rather than the transfer time alone.

Start = `--teal-600` fill + `#fff`; Cancel = `1px solid var(--rule)` (clone `Dropzone.tsx:229-262`).
**Amber is the approval gate's alone (BRAND §2)** — the refusal panel uses the established failure
palette (`#fef2f2`/`#fecaca`/`#991b1b`), not `--held`.

**State lives in `VaultPage`, not `VaultBody`** — Refresh remounts `VaultBody` via the `nonce` key and
would silently discard a picked 1.5 GB selection.

The reserve can refuse with fresher numbers than the card showed (the card is read at T, reserve
happens at T+8min of transfer). **Design the Start failure state for that** — it is not an edge case.
