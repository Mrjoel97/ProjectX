# Phase 50 provider preflight — Stripe + PayPal owner preference

Checked 2026-09-24 against official provider pages. This is a read-only decision aid, **not** an accepted provider/account policy or merchant eligibility certificate.

| Question | Current official evidence | Implementation consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All-country seller onboarding? | [Stripe global availability](https://stripe.com/global) lists supported business countries/regions and distinguishes selling to buyers worldwide from a seller having a Stripe account. It does not list Tanzania. [PayPal seller onboarding](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/seller-onboarding) marks Tanzania “Send only” and says send-only countries cannot receive payments through partner integrations. | The owner's “all countries” seller target is presently unmet with these two paths. Provider-specific seller-country/account capability must be versioned and unknown/unsupported combinations refused. Buyer reach is a different question. |
| Does country alone prove PayPal eligibility? | [PayPal seller onboarding](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/seller-onboarding) explicitly indexes country by seller account type, before/after-payment onboarding and PayPal Checkout versus Expanded Checkout. [Stripe connected-account configuration](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2/connected-account-configuration) makes its account configuration explicit. | Decision rows and operation approvals must match account type/configuration, onboarding flow and checkout product as well as account, country, method and currencies. No flow may inherit another's eligibility. |
| USD, EUR, GBP settlement? | [PayPal currency codes](https://developer.paypal.com/reference/currency-codes/) include USD, EUR and GBP, but receiving/holding can depend on account preferences and country-specific offerings. Stripe's global page alone does not certify the three settlement currencies for every seller country/account. | Quote currency and actual settlement currency must be separately pinned to verified merchant-account capabilities; do not assume all three for every account. |
| Merchant account model and seller onboarding? | [PayPal onboarding checklist](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/seller-onboarding/onboarding-checklist/) says permitted seller account types, products and features depend on the partner REST app configuration and recommends discussing them with an account manager. [Stripe global availability](https://stripe.com/global) does not by itself establish Connect-platform approval or cross-border payout topology. | The owner selected merchant-owned accounts; the exact delegated connection, fees/liability and provider eligibility for each remain decision gates. No provider applications, credentials or onboarding should be inferred from public docs. |

The owner has since chosen **merchant-owned direct accounts**. This specifies who owns the receiving account; it does not mean Pikar-AI can process payments without a provider-supported delegated connection. [Stripe's Standard-account OAuth guide](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/oauth-standard-accounts) explicitly says OAuth is **not recommended for new Connect platforms** and points to [Connect Onboarding for Standard accounts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/standard-accounts). Stripe's [connected-account configuration](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2/connected-account-configuration) makes fee collection and negative-balance liability explicit properties that cannot simply be inferred from the phrase “merchant-owned.” [PayPal's seller-onboarding overview](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/seller-onboarding) identifies before-payment partner onboarding as the common route for a hosted platform and requires the seller to grant permission before processing. The final decision must pin the exact approved integration/account flow for each provider, not assume raw merchant API-key collection or Pikar-AI-controlled pooled funds.

Owner inputs are recorded separately in `50-06-OWNER-INPUTS-2026-09-24.md`: **both Stripe and PayPal are required in the first test-mode release**, each merchant owns its direct provider accounts, all seller countries are targeted, and USD/EUR/GBP settlement is targeted. Plans 50-06–17 have been replanned for independent Stripe and PayPal adapters, explicit per-merchant eligible provider/account selection, global dual-provider first-release qualification and separate actual sandbox evidence; independent plan-checker review passed, while provider/owner facts remain pending before adapter execution or public opening.

Other decision gates remain open: registered entity and account eligibility, seller/fee/liability posture, supported payment methods by account/country, tax/shipping/refund authority, stock/oversell policy, notification and fulfilment policy, buyer/order retention, exact sandbox and production activation authority. Public commerce stays closed.
