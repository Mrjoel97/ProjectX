<objective>
**SEAM 2 of 2 — the `spine` return field.** Add `spine: string | null` to `vaultGroundHydrated`'s
return, **alongside** `docIds`/`titles`/`chunks` and **never inside them**, and have the two
grounding-driven callers consume it explicitly.

Purpose: this is the half of the design that protects the cockpit. Research proved that prepending
the blueprint as entry 0 breaks `llm.ts` three ways — the honest no-match answer becomes
structurally unreachable (HIGH), every `vault.searched` count inflates, and a "Business blueprint"
chip appears on every search — and that `voiceDoc.ts`'s `docIds[i] !== docRef` filter would have
silently discarded it anyway. A separate field fixes all four at once, and requires **zero edits to
`llm.ts`**. That "zero edits" claim is the thing this plan must prove, not assert.

This plan is deliberately separate from plan 06 (the cockpit turn prompt) and **must not be
conflated with it**.

Output: one field, two explicit consumers, and the regression guards that pin the whole decision.
</objective>
