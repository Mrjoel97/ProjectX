## What was wrong

`plans.persistDeck`'s `artDirection` arg validator (`packages/backend/convex/plans.ts:367-380`)
omitted `music`. `parseArtDirection` emits it; `schema.ts:767` stores it. Convex `v.object` is
closed, so the mutation threw `ArgumentValidationError` and `runMedia` died **after**
`dispatchAndLand` had already landed the memo — leaving the plan at `kind: "memo"` carrying the
model's raw prose, with **no `media.deck_*` audit terminal written at all**.

Introduced by `e2b281f` ("the music bed"), which widened the parser, the schema, the price table,
the renderer and the skill body — and never `plans.ts`.

### The placement is the load-bearing part, and it is a vacuity trap

`persistSceneDeck` is handed `variations.a.body` — the slice **between** the two `VARIATION`
headings — not the whole body. So an art-direction section placed above `## VARIATION A` parses to
`null`, and **every assertion below it passes with or without the fix**. The fixture rides the
section *inside* variation A on purpose, and asserts `palette` first to prove the section was read
off variation A's slice at all.

Until this plan, `dispatch.ts:886` — the SCENE path, the one production runs and the one the owner
hit live on 2026-08-30 — had never been exercised with a non-null `artDirection`.

---

## The fix

One optional field on the arg validator, with the comment naming why it was missed:

```ts
music: v.optional(v.string()),
```

`schema.ts` was **not** touched — it already had the field. `parseArtDirection` was **not** touched.
The defect was exactly one missing line.
