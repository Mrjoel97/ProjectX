## The two costs, recorded rather than buried

**Counterparty.** ADR-026's decisive argument was that Veo added no new data-transfer counterparty.
ADR-027 reverses that *argument*, not merely its conclusion — xAI becomes a new recipient of
customer scene prompts, and OpenRouter does not launder that (OpenRouter was already approved for
text; xAI has never received anything from this product). The ADR records that the owner was shown
this and chose Grok in exchange for the `1–15` grid, because the value of the record is that a later
reader can distinguish an accepted cost from an overlooked one.

**The guarantee.** 15 s = $1.05 and 30 s = $2.10 all-generated, both under the cap, both previously
impossible. Three artifacts encode the dying claim and are named for 33.1-04/06.
`MEDIA_GENERATED_SECONDS_CAP = 12` is the mitigation — and the ADR says plainly that a ceiling in
code is **not** an impossibility in arithmetic: the old one could not be switched off, the new one
is a constant somebody can raise in a check somebody can move, and planning already found one route
around it (a legacy BLOCK deck).
