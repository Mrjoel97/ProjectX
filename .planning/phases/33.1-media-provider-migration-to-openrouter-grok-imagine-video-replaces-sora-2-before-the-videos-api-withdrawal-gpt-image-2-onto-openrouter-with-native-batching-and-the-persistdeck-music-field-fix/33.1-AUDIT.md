## CONFIRMED — the stale cost ratio, found independently by TWO lenses

**33.1-03 repriced a still from `$0.01` to a measured `$0.006`. It updated the cost tables and the
`packages/cost` assertion (`200/3` = 66.7×). It did not update anything a human or a model READS.**

The true clip-vs-still lever is now **66.7×** (`$0.40` vs `$0.006` at 4 s). Every surface below still
says 40×, understating by ~40% **the only cost lever this product tells anyone they have.**

**The three tests pin the stale number.** That is the defect, not a side effect of it: the price
could move again and nothing would go red, because the guards assert the old literal rather than the
computed ratio. A check that cannot fail, over a money claim.

**The skill body is the sharpest layer.** It does not merely misinform a reader — it tells the model
stills are "forty times cheaper" and instructs it to "reach past a `generated_video`" on that basis.
The number steering every deck's kind-mix is wrong.

**Third occurrence of this exact defect.** `media.md:3876` records it as "a tenth" until 33-06, then
"a fortieth" — and 3877 says *"All three sites now say fortieth."* The sites were corrected twice and
the mechanism that let them rot was never fixed: the string **restates** a computed value instead of
**deriving** it.

### Required fix — and it is NOT "change 40 to 67"

**Derive the ratio from the tables.** A literal restating a computed value is what rotted twice.
`sceneVisualSpec("generated_video", 4).usd / sceneVisualSpec("animated_image", 4).usd`, rounded, is
the number — computed at render, not typed. The tests then assert *against the same derivation*, so a
price move updates the sentence and cannot silently disagree with it.

  `packages/cost`. It takes the three UI strings, `mediaCanvas.test.ts`, and the e2e literal.
- **Plan 33.1-06** owns the skill body. It takes the four `mediaDirector.ts` sites — and note the body

## REFUTED — recorded so they are not re-raised

**"The `$0.006` row under-reserves for a long prompt."** Refuted on the real path, and the refutation
is worth keeping: `estCents = Math.max(1, Math.ceil(est.value * 100))`, so a standalone image reserves
**one integer cent** either way. The `0.01 → 0.006` change moved the reserved amount by **exactly zero
cents**. Worst-case vendor charge `$0.00974` < `$0.01` reserved.

**"`regenerateBlock`'s partial buy routes around `MEDIA_GENERATED_SECONDS_CAP`."** Refuted only
because the cap **does not exist yet** — plan 04 has not run. The narrowing at `media.ts:502-506` is
real. **Plan 04 must re-test this once the cap lands**; this refutation expires with that commit.
