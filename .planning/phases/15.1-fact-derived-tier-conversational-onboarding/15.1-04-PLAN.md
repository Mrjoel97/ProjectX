<objective>
Remove the LAST authoritative reader of the persona string in the profile markdown. `evaluations.ts`
picks its rubric from the `tenantProfiles` table instead.

Purpose: SC#2 says a malformed doc "can no longer silently reclassify a tenant". The design doc is
explicit (§4.2) that this is achieved by removing the last authoritative reader, NOT by changing
`deserializeProfile`'s fallback — the fallback *"stops being a silent reclassification risk once
nothing authoritative depends on it"*. This plan is what makes that sentence true.
Output: defect 1d closed on the read side.
</objective>
