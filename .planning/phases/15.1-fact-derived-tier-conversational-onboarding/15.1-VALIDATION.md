# Phase 15.1 — Validation Strategy

> **Closure reconciliation (2026-08-16):** Plans 15.1-01 through 15.1-07 and
> `15.1-VERIFICATION.md` close all six success criteria; every Wave 0 asset below exists. The live
> backfill, profile, and conversational-turn rows were paid and are recorded in `deferred-items.md`.
> The specialist-output row was run but remains inconclusive: the solopreneur and SME prompts
> differed, while the two sampled `money-model-designer` outputs did not visibly differ. Prompt
> shaping therefore does **not** itself prove visibly different model output. This does not reopen
> ADR-009's locked prompt-shaping contract, but it remains an explicit evidence caveat.

> Per-phase validation contract for feedback sampling during execution.
> Derived from `15.1-RESEARCH.md` § Validation Architecture. Read that section for the reasoning
> behind each row.

**Environment constraint, material to every row below:** this worktree
(`.worktrees/lane-a-dispatch`) has **no live `CONVEX_DEPLOYMENT`**. Offline gates work
(`convex-test` + vitest, `tsc --noEmit`, `scripts/check-playbooks.mjs`). Anything needing a live
backend or a real model call does not. The backfill migration in particular **cannot be run for
real** here — only exercised through `convex-test`'s in-memory DB; the operator `migrations.runner`
invocation is a live-deployment step deferred to integration on `main`.
