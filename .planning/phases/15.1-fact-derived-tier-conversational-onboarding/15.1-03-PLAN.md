<objective>
The subtraction. Remove the tier as an INPUT from every layer that accepts one today — the Convex arg
validator, the domain validator, both pages, and the extraction skill's instruction to guess — and make
the two audit rows tell the truth.

Purpose: this is defect 1b, and the design doc is explicit that a UI-only fix *"hides the button and
leaves the control"*. It is also where the "markdown is a projection, the table is the record"
invariant becomes real: `commitProfile`/`updateProfile` read the tier from `tenantProfiles` and splice
it into the serialized doc.
Output: no caller-supplied tier anywhere, truthful audit payloads, a fail-closed completion gate.
</objective>
