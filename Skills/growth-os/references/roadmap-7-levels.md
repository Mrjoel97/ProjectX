# The 7-Level Roadmap (Zero to $100M)

From $100M Leads. Locates a business and sets its **primary action**. Growth OS uses the level to sanity-check the prescription — the constraint should match the level.

| Level | You have reached it when | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Your friends know about your stuff. One offer, one avatar, one platform. | **Warm outreach.** |
| **2** | You consistently reach everyone you know; you know the exact inputs per engaged lead. | Max warm outreach + post as much content as you can. |
| **3** | You get employees to do more advertising. | Hire people to advertise profitably on your behalf. |
| **4** | Product is good enough for consistent referrals (aim >= 25% of customers from referrals). | Fix product until referrals are consistent, then scale advertising with a bigger team. |
| **5** | You advertise in more places/ways/people. | Profitably use >= 2 methods on multiple platforms (New placements → platforms → activities). |
| **6** | You hire "killers" — experienced executives run advertising departments without you. | Battle-hardened executives/department heads take over channels. |
| **7** | Reserved ("once I cross a billion"). | — |

## Using level in diagnosis
- Levels 1–2: the constraint is almost always **offer** (is it worth buying?) or **volume of warm/owned outreach**. Don't prescribe paid-ads scaling here.
- Levels 3–4: monetization and **product quality → referrals** dominate. money-model-designer + the referral playbook.
- Levels 5–6: **lead-engine scaling** (More/Better/New, Lead Getters, paid ads at scale).

## The mindset (Many-Sided Die)
Roll green as many times as you can; every green turns a red side green (compounding skill + luck). "You cannot lose if you do not quit." Growth OS encodes this as: keep the loop running — diagnose, fix one constraint, measure, repeat.
