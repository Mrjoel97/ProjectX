# Lead Magnets — a complete solution to a narrow problem

A **lead magnet** is *a complete solution to a narrow problem*. You give it away to
turn strangers and passive contacts into **engaged leads**. The engine behind it:
*a person who pays with their time now is more likely to pay with their money later.*

## The 4 things a good lead magnet does
1. **Engages your ideal customers** (attracts the right people, not everyone).
2. **Gets more of them to engage** than your core offer would (lower commitment).
3. Is **valuable enough to consume** (they actually use it, not just grab it).
4. Makes the right people **more likely to buy** your core offer afterward.

## The Problem–Solution Cycle
Every solution reveals the next problem. Pick a problem that is **narrow AND
meaningful**, solve it completely, and make sure **the next problem it reveals is one
your core offer solves.** That way consuming the magnet naturally sets up the sale.

## The 7 Steps to build one
1. **Figure out the problem + who it's for.** Narrow and meaningful; tied to your core offer.
2. **Decide how to solve it — pick a TYPE (3):**
   - **a) Reveal Their Problem** (a diagnosis) — e.g. a speed test, a posture analysis.
   - **b) Samples & Trials** — brief full access to the real thing.
   - **c) One Step of a Multi-Step Process** — do the first step free (e.g. "first coat free").
3. **Decide how to deliver it — pick a METHOD (4):**
   - **a) Software** (calculator, dashboard, tool)
   - **b) Information** (course, interview, checklist, tips)
   - **c) Services** (an audit, an adjustment, done-for-you piece)
   - **d) Physical Products** (a sample, a book, swag)
   > 3 types x 4 methods = **up to 12 versions** of the same idea. Try more than one.
4. **Test the name.** Test elements **in this order: (1) headline, (2) image(s),
   (3) subheadline.** Ogilvy: the headline is *"80 cents of your dollar."*
5. **Make it easy to consume** in whatever format the audience prefers.
6. **Make it darn good.** *Give Away The Secrets, Sell The Implementation.* Over-deliver
   on the magnet; the core offer is where you help them execute.
7. **Make it easy to say they want more — the CTA.** A CTA = **(1) what to do +
   (2) reasons to do it now.**

## The CTA — "reasons now" (3 tools)
- **Scarcity** — real, limited capacity ("only 7 spots").
- **Urgency** — a real deadline ("closes Friday").
- **Fraternity Party Planner** — give a *"because"* reason (any honest reason to act now).
- Rule for the whole CTA: **"Don't be clever, be clear."** Spell out click / call / reply.

## Economics
A good lead magnet cuts CAC dramatically — worked example: **CAC ~$3,000 → ~$1,000
(about 3x cheaper)** — because it filters for buyers and warms them before the ask.
It also feeds the referral and affiliate machines (they can give it away for you).
