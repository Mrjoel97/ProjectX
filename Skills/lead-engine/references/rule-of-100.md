# The Rule of 100 & Open To Goal — do the reps

Advertising always works; only efficiency varies. The failure mode is almost never
the channel — it's not doing **enough** of it. Two commitment devices fix that.

## The Rule of 100 (MORE)
Do **100 primary actions per day for 100 days** on ONE channel before you judge it.
The primary action is different per channel:

| Channel          | 100/day means…                                        |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Warm Outreach**| 100 reach-outs per day                                |
| **Post Content** | 100 minutes/day creating + **>= 1 post per platform** |
| **Cold Outreach**| 100 reach-outs per day                                |
| **Paid Ads**     | 100 minutes/day making ads + **run for 100 days**     |

Track pace with `../scripts/rule_of_100_tracker.py` (progress %, on-track/behind,
days remaining to 100).

## Open To Goal (the Rule of 100 on steroids)
Instead of committing to an INPUT (100 actions), commit to an OUTCOME and work until
you hit it — **"5 new members per day, no matter what."** You don't stop for the day
until the number is reached. This forces you to do *whatever it takes* rather than
clock a quota.

## Habit stack (make the reps happen)
- Wake **4–5am**.
- Go **right to work** (advertising first, before anything reactive).
- **No meetings until noon** — protect the maker block for lead generation.

## The One-Page Advertising Checklist (5 steps)
1. **Pick the engaged-lead type** to focus on: Customers / Affiliates / Employees / Agencies.
2. **Pick your commitment:** Rule of 100 **or** Open To Goal.
3. **Fill out the advertising checklist** (offer + channel + daily action + benchmark).
   Template: `../assets/advertising-checklist.template.md`.
4. **Do it daily** until you can afford to pay someone else to do it.
5. **Then make employees the new target** (hire a lead-getter) and repeat the checklist.
