# The Core Four — the only four ways to get engaged leads

Every way to let people know what you sell is one of four activities. They are the
2x2 of **who you're talking to** (Warm = people who gave you permission / Cold =
strangers) crossed with **how many at once** (1-to-1 / 1-to-many).

|                | 1-to-1 (private)     | 1-to-many (broadcast) |
|----------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| **Warm** (permission) | **Warm Outreach**   | **Post Free Content** |
| **Cold** (strangers)  | **Cold Outreach**   | **Paid Ads**          |

- **Warm Outreach** — reach out privately to people who already know you (your list).
- **Post Free Content** — publish to an audience that opted in to hear from you.
- **Cold Outreach** — contact strangers privately (DM, email, phone).
- **Paid Ads** — pay a platform to show your message to strangers at scale.

## Warm vs Cold, 1-to-1 vs 1-to-many
- **Warm** means the person gave you permission to contact them (they're a contact,
  a follower, a past lead). **Cold** means they're a stranger who never opted in.
- **1-to-1** is private and personalized; it converts higher per contact but takes
  your time. **1-to-many** is one message to many; lower response rate per person
  but far more reach per unit of effort.

## The decision rule (which one to start with)
1. **Always start with Warm Outreach.** You already have permission; it's free and
   it works fastest. Your first ~1,000 leads usually come from people you know.
2. Then split by your scarcest resource:
   - **More time than money → Post Free Content** (compounding, unpaid, slow to start).
   - **More money than time → Cold Outreach or Paid Ads** (buy reach / speed now).
3. **Pick ONE and max it** before adding another (see `more-better-new.md`: More →
   Better → New). Doing one channel well beats dabbling in four.

## The offer you attach to every channel (Value Equation fill-in)
A channel only produces engaged leads if it carries an offer people want. Build the
offer on the Value Equation — **maximize** Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood of
Achievement, **minimize** Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice — then state it as:

> "I help **(ideal customer)** get **(dream outcome)** in **(time period)** without
> **(effort and sacrifice)** and **(increase perceived likelihood)**."

Fill that sentence before you pick up the phone, hit record, or launch an ad. The
channel is the delivery truck; this sentence is the cargo.
