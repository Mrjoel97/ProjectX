# The Four Lead Getters — get OTHER people to advertise for you

The Core Four are things **you** do. The Four Lead Getters are **people who do the
Core Four for you** — that's leverage. You still do the Core Four; you just do it to
*recruit and equip lead-getters* instead of chasing leads yourself.

## Leverage (4 scenarios)
1. **You** get leads (Core Four yourself).
2. You get **one** person to get leads for you.
3. You get **many** people to get leads for you.
4. You get **one person who gets many others** to get leads for you.
Each step up multiplies output without multiplying your hours.

## The Four Lead Getters (adoption order)
Do them in this order — each needs the previous to be working first:
1. **Customers** (referrals) — happy customers send you more customers.
2. **Employees** — you hire people to run the Core Four.
3. **Agencies** — you rent expertise to learn from (not depend on).
4. **Affiliates** — other businesses promote you to their audiences for a cut.

## #1 Customer Referrals
- **Referral Growth Equation:** Referrals in − Churned out.
  - referrals **>** churn → you grow for **free**.
  - referrals **=** churn → you need ads to grow.
  - referrals **<** churn → you advertise just to break even.
  - When referrals beat churn it compounds exponentially (1 → 2 → 4 → 8).
- **Goodwill = Value − Price.** You earn referrals by giving more value than you charge.
- **6 ways to give more value** (each maps to a part of a great ad):
  1. **Call Outs → Sell Better Customers** (the right customers refer more).
  2. **Dream Outcome → Set Better Expectations** (deliver what you promised).
  3. **Likelihood → Get More People Better Results** (the 6-step results process).
  4. **Time Delay → Faster Wins** — BAMFAM (Book-A-Meeting-From-A-Meeting), get a win
     in 48 hrs, and pad quoted timelines by ~50% so you beat them.
  5. **Effort & Sacrifice → Keep Improving the Product** (a monthly 6-step improvement loop).
  6. **CTA → Tell Them What To Buy Next.**
- **7 ways to ask for referrals:**
  1. One-Sided Benefit (reward the referrer).
  2. Two-Sided (both sides win — Dropbox, PayPal).
  3. Ask **at purchase** ("who else could you do this with?").
  4. Negotiation Chip (a discount in exchange for 3 intros).
  5. Referral Events.
  6. Ongoing Programs.
  7. Unlockable Bonuses.
  > Dropbox's two-sided referral **39x'd the company in 15 months.**
- Build a referral program: `../playbooks/07-referrals.md`.

## #2 Employees
- **Hire them with the internal Core Four:**
  - Warm = **Ask Your Network**
  - Cold = **Recruiting** (reach out to strangers)
  - Content = **Post Job Openings**
  - Paid = **Promote the Postings**
- **Train with the 3 Ds:** **Document** (write the checklist) → **Demonstrate** (show
  it done) → **Duplicate** (they do it, you correct the checklist).
  *"If they get it wrong, YOU got the checklist wrong."*
- **Returns / diagnostic:** Total Payroll ÷ Total Engaged Leads = cost per lead;
  × leads-per-customer = CAC; LTGP ÷ CAC = ratio. If **CAC > 3x industry average**,
  ask: *"do the leads have the problem AND the money?"* — No → advertising problem;
  Yes but not buying → **sales** problem.
- Hire/train a lead-getter: `../playbooks/09-employees.md`.

## #3 Agencies — use them to LEARN, not to depend
- **The "Right Way" agreement:** work with an agency ~6 months to **learn**, pay extra
  to have them break down their decisions, then **train your own team**, then move to
  low-cost consulting. The goal is skill transfer, not permanent outsourcing.
- **Pick an agency (10-point checklist):** (1) known good results; (2) prominent
  clients; (3) a waiting list; (4) a clear process with realistic expectations;
  (5) plays long-term (no hacks); (6) tells you what THEY need from you; (7) a regular
  schedule; (8) simple updates; (9) a good offer (built on the Value Equation);
  (10) expensive (cheap agencies cost more in the end).
- Engage an agency: `../playbooks/10-agencies.md`.

## #4 Affiliates (6 steps)
1. **Find ideal affiliates** — ask *"Who already has my leads?"* (softwares, products,
   equipment, services, groups, events serving the same customer).
2. **Make them an offer** (Call Out + Value + CTA): "Make more money from your current
   customers + get more leads… without building, delivering, or supporting it…
   starting tomorrow."
3. **Qualify them** (get them invested): Make Them a Customer / Make Them an Expert.
   Charge **10–20%** of what the average active affiliate makes in their first 12 months.
4. **What to pay — a 3-tier payout** (example $40 max CAC):
   - Tier 1: **25% ($10)** on sign-up.
   - Tier 2: **50% ($20)** on activation.
   - Tier 3: **100% ($40)** on sustain.
   - Blended payout stays **below your max CAC.**
5. **Get them advertising — Whisper-Tease-Shout launch cadence** (see affiliates playbook).
6. **Keep them advertising — Integrate** three ways: (1) affiliates **give away** your
   lead magnet; (2) affiliates **sell** your lead magnet; (3) affiliates **sell** your
   core offer.
- **Returns:** cost to get an affiliate vs GP of the customers they send. If ratio < 3:
  lower CAC / raise LTGP + lower CAC / raise LTGP.
- Build an affiliate program: `../playbooks/08-affiliates.md`.
