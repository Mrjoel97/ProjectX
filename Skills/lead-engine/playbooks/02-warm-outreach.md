# Playbook 02 — Warm Outreach (the 10 steps)

Reach out privately to people who already gave you permission. Warm + 1-to-1. This is
almost always your first channel and your first ~1,000 leads.

## Inputs
- Every contact you have (phone, email, every social platform).
- Your Value Equation offer sentence.
- A free version of your offer you can give the first five people.

## The 10 steps
1. **Get your list.** Export ALL contacts — phone, email, every social. This is often
   your first 1,000 leads.
2. **Pick a platform** — the one where you have the most contacts. Start there.
3. **Personalize the greeting** — use their name and something real about them.
4. **Reach out — 100 people every day.** Follow up **up to 3 times** if no answer.
5. **Warm them up with A-C-A:** **Acknowledge** (something true about them) →
   **Compliment** (genuine) → **Ask** (about them, not you). Have a real conversation first.
6. **Make the offer using the Value Equation** — and ask the magic question:
   **"Do you know anybody who…?"** (This lets them refer without feeling sold to, and
   many will raise their own hand.)
7. **Make it FREE for the first five.** Give it free in exchange for a promise to
   (1) use it, (2) give feedback, (3) leave a review. If they say no, ask:
   **"What would I have to do to make it worth it?"**
8. **Start at the top of the next platform** and repeat steps 2–7.
9. **Start charging** once it works: free → **80% off → 60% off → raise ~20% every five**
   customers until you reach full price.
10. **Keep the list warm** with Dean Jackson's **9-word email:**
    *"Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"*

## Benchmarks (expected conversion)
- ~**1 in 5** you reach engage.
- ~**1 in 5** of those take the free offer.
- ~**1 in 4** of those convert to paying.
- Net: **~1 customer per 100 reach-outs.**
- Rule of thumb: **1% of your list** buys a ~$400 offer. Reach out to **500/week →
  ~5 customers/week → ~$104,000/yr.**

## Artifact
A **warm-outreach plan:** list count, chosen starting platform, the A-C-A script, the
offer + "do you know anybody" ask, the first-five-free script, and daily target (100/day).
