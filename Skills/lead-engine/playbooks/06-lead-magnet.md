# Playbook 06 — Build a Lead Magnet

A lead magnet is *a complete solution to a narrow problem* you give away to turn
passive people into engaged leads. Reference: `../references/lead-magnets.md`. Fill-in
template: `../assets/lead-magnet.template.md`.

## Inputs
- Your core offer (so the magnet's "next problem" leads into it).
- Your ideal customer.

## The 7 steps
1. **Figure out the problem + who it's for.** Make it **narrow AND meaningful**, and
   make sure the next problem it reveals is one your **core offer solves**
   (the Problem–Solution Cycle).
2. **Pick the TYPE (1 of 3):**
   - **Reveal Their Problem** (a diagnosis — speed test, posture analysis), or
   - **Samples & Trials** (brief full access), or
   - **One Step of a Multi-Step Process** ("first coat free").
3. **Pick the DELIVERY METHOD (1 of 4):** Software / Information / Services / Physical
   Products. (3 types × 4 methods = up to **12 versions** — consider several.)
4. **Test the name** — test in order: **(1) headline → (2) image(s) → (3) subheadline.**
   The headline is ~"80 cents of your dollar" (Ogilvy).
5. **Make it easy to consume** in its format.
6. **Make it darn good** — *Give Away The Secrets, Sell The Implementation.*
7. **Make it easy to say they want more — the CTA** = (1) what to do + (2) reasons now.
   Reasons-now tools: **Scarcity** (real capacity), **Urgency** (deadline),
   **Fraternity Party Planner** (a "because" reason). *"Don't be clever, be clear."*

## Economics check
A good magnet cuts CAC ~3x (worked example **$3,000 → $1,000**). It also plugs into the
Core Four (offer it in outreach/content/ads) and the Lead Getters (affiliates give it away).

## Artifact
A **specified lead magnet:** narrow problem + who; chosen type; delivery method; name
(tested headline / image / subheadline); the CTA (what-to-do + reason-now). Use
`../assets/lead-magnet.template.md`.
