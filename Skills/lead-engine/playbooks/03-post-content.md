# Playbook 03 — Post Free Content

Publish to an audience that opted in. Warm + 1-to-many. Best when you have more time
than money. Every post is one **content unit: Hook → Retain → Reward.**

## Inputs
- One platform to start (where your audience already is).
- Your Value Equation offer (for the occasional ask).
- 100 minutes/day to create + ≥1 post/platform (Rule of 100).

## Step 1 — Build the content unit: Hook → Retain → Reward
- **HOOK (3 levers) — get the click/stop:**
  1. **Topic** — pick from 5: Far Past, Recent Past, Present, Trending, Manufactured.
  2. **Headline** — use **≥2 of 7** headline elements: Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity,
     Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing.
  3. **Format** — match the platform's native format.
- **RETAIN (curiosity) — keep them watching/reading:** use **Lists, Steps, Stories.**
- **REWARD — pay off their attention:** maximize value per second. *"There's no such
  thing as too long, only too boring."* Test of success: **the audience grows.**

## Step 2 — Set the Give:Ask ratio
- Mature ratio ≈ **3.5 : 1** (TV runs ~47 give / ~13 ask). **"Give until they ask"** —
  keep giving free value until the audience starts asking to buy.
- **Give in public, ask in private.**
- **Two ask strategies:**
  - **Integrated** — a small ad in every piece; keeps the ratio high. Best for **long-form.**
  - **Intermittent** — many gives, then one clear ask. Best for **short-form.**

## Step 3 — Scale
- **Depth then Width** or **Width then Depth** — go deeper on one platform, or wider
  across platforms. Master one before adding the next.

## The 7 content lessons
1. **"How to" → "How I"** (share your specific story, not generic advice).
2. **Remind more than you teach** (people need reminders, not just new info).
3. **Puddles → Ponds → Lakes → Oceans** (start narrow, expand your audience over time).
4. **Content = sales tools** (repurpose posts to close leads 1-to-1).
5. **Free content retains** (it keeps existing customers, not just gets new ones).
6. **Higher standards, not shorter attention** (make it better, don't make it shorter).
7. **Avoid pre-scheduling** (stay responsive to what's working now).

## Benchmarks
- **How big:** monthly total followers / reach.
- **How fast:** the growth *rate* (New / Started this period).

## Artifact
A **content plan:** platform, hook formula (topic + ≥2 headline elements + format),
retain device, reward standard, Give:Ask ratio + strategy, and daily cadence.
