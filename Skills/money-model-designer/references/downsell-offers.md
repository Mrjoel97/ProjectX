# Downsell Offers — get a yes from someone about to say no (Stage II)

**"Any offer after someone says no is a downsell."** Two axes: change **how they pay**
(Payment Plan, Trial With Penalty) or change **what they get** (Feature Downsell).

## The 6 rules of downselling
1. They said no to **THIS** (this offer, not you) — keep going.
2. Downsells are **trades**, not concessions.
3. **Personalize, don't pressure.**
4. Offer the **same things in new ways.**
5. **DON'T drop the price for the same thing** — that's discounting, not downselling.
6. **Test prices in advance** so you know your floors.

## 1. Payment Plan Downsell (7 steps — stop as soon as they buy)
1. **Reward paying in full** — present the price with interest ($15) and offer a prepay
   price ($10).
2. **3rd-party financing** / credit / layaway.
3. **Half now, half later.**
4. **Check if they still want it** (scale 1-10): **8+** keep offering payment plans;
   **7 or below** switch to a Feature Downsell.
5. **Split into three payments.**
6. **Evenly spread** across the full term.
7. **Free Trial.**

- **The Seesaw:** ask *"giant monthly payments or tiny ones?"* Align charge dates to
  their **paycheck dates**. Re-run declined cards the **same day** (~**1/3 recovered**).
- **Churn by cadence (Profitwell):** Monthly **10.7%** / Quarterly **5%** / Annual **2%**
  monthly cancellation. Longer cadence = far less churn.

## 2. Trial With Penalty (5 steps — for recurring businesses)
The inverse of Win Your Money Back: they pay **only if they DON'T meet the terms.**
1. **Offer the trial last** (after other downsells).
2. **Always get a card.**
3. **Sell staying and paying** (frame it as the good outcome).
4. **Explain the fees AFTER the card** is on file.
5. **Check-ins are required** (they enforce the terms).
- **Fee structures:** per-miss (e.g. $50 each) or lump ($500).
- **Post-trial branches:** likes it -> auto-billing; hates it -> take the blame, offer a
  better fit ("**half buy**"); didn't use it -> waive the fee, get them back on track.

## 3. Feature Downsell (change what they get)
"**Take something away, lower the price, ask 'how about now?'**" Remove features
**highest-to-lowest value** so they **re-upsell themselves** to add them back.

**The 4 levers:**
1. **Quantity** — give less.
2. **Quality** — lower grade / tier.
3. **Service Quality features** — time/location availability, cancellations, speed of
   response/delivery, 1:1 vs 1-to-many, communication method, staff qualifications, live
   vs recorded, in-person vs remote, DIY / DWY / DFY, personalization, insurance/guarantee.
4. **Remove entire features.**

- **Name the tiers:** most expensive = "**The Whale**" / "High Roller"; cheapest = "**The
  Minimum.**"
- After **2 downsells**, run a **temperature check**: **8+** -> move to a payment plan;
  **7 or below** -> ask *"what would a 10 look like?"*
- Close with "**Deal?**" / "**Fair enough?**" **Never negotiate price** for the same thing.

## Choosing among the 3
- They want it but can't pay all at once, intent is high (8+) -> **Payment Plan**.
- Recurring business, they're hesitant to commit -> **Trial With Penalty**.
- Intent has dropped (7-) or budget is the wall -> **Feature Downsell**.
