# Upsell Offers — get customers to spend more (Stage II)

An upsell **solves the next problem the purchase reveals.** (McDonald's: the burger
makes ~$0.25 profit -> fries -> drink -> supersize.) Upsells **fail** when they're the
**wrong thing, at the wrong time, in the wrong way.** Offer **More / Better / New.**

## Universal upsell tactics (apply to all four)
- Offer the **more profitable** thing first.
- **"Say no to say yes"** — let them decline one thing so the next yes is easier.
- **Surprise & Delight** existing customers.
- **Hyper Buying Cycle** — sell more right after a purchase, when buying intent is highest.
- **BAMFAM = Book-A-Meeting-From-A-Meeting** — never leave a touchpoint without the
  next one scheduled.
- Charge **5-50%** of the price for guarantees/warranties.
- **Price-nudge (decoy pricing)** to push customers to the Large:

  | Size | Price |
  |---|---|
  | Small | $5 |
  | Medium | $8 (not $7 — the "wrong" price nudges them up) |
  | Large | $9 |

The 4 upsell offers:

## 1. Classic Upsell
"You can't have X **without** Y." (Car wash -> paint sealant.) The purchase creates a
new need; the upsell fills it.

## 2. Menu Upsell — 4 tactics, in order
1. **Unsell** — tell them what they **don't** need (builds trust).
2. **Prescription Upsell** — tell them what they **do** need, spoken "as if they already
   have it."
3. **A/B Upsell** — offer **A or B**, never "whether to buy."
4. **Card On File** — keep the payment method ready for the next yes.

**The "Economist" Play:** present **3 options where the bundle (C) is priced the same as
the more expensive single (B)**:
- Digital $59 / Print $125 / **Both $125** -> almost everyone takes **C**.

## 3. Anchor Upsell (5 steps)
1. **Present the anchor** — a premium option priced **5-10x** the main offer.
2. **Get "The Gasp"** — the sticker-shock reaction.
3. **Come to the rescue** — pivot to the sensible option.
4. **Present the main offer** (now it looks reasonable).
5. **Ask how they pay.**
- The main and premium options must **share the same primary features.**
- **Actually sell the anchor** — some people buy it; it must be real.

## 4. Rollover Upsell
Credit a prior purchase toward a more expensive next offer.
- **WHO:** win-back (dormant 6+ months) / your upset customers / competitors' upset
  customers / your regulars.
- **WHAT:** More / Better / New.
- **HOW:** all or part of the credit; applied up front or spread over time.
- **Rule:** make the upsell **>= 4x the credit** (a full credit = at most a 25% discount).
- **"Famous" Gift Card:** sell $200 gift cards for $20, usable only on your other offers.

## Choosing among the 4
- The purchase creates an obvious dependent need -> **Classic**.
- You have several add-ons and want trust + choice -> **Menu** (+ Economist play).
- You want a big-ticket option to reset the price frame -> **Anchor**.
- You want to re-activate past buyers or steal upset competitors -> **Rollover**.
