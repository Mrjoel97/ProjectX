# The Four Offer Types + Sequencing

Every Money Model is assembled from four offer types. Each does one job.

| Type | One-line job | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| **Attraction** | Turn strangers into customers | I — Get Cash |
| **Upsell** | Get customers to spend more | II — Get More Cash |
| **Downsell** | Get a yes from someone about to say no | II — Get More Cash |
| **Continuity** | Keep them buying (ongoing value for ongoing payment) | III — Get The Most Cash |

- **Attraction** = strangers into customers.
- **Upsell** = spend more.
- **Downsell** = say yes when they'd otherwise say no.
- **Continuity** = keep buying.

## Stacking logic
Each type stacks 30-day cash on top of the last:
- Attraction brings in the customer and covers the cost of getting them.
- Upsell raises the profit per customer above that cost (this is where "getting paid
  to get customers" is won).
- Downsell recovers the customers who said no to the upsell — a trade, not a discount.
- Continuity captures the last sale inside the 30-day window **and** the recurring
  lifetime value after it.

## Sequencing (the order a customer experiences)
```
advertise -> attraction -> upsell -> downsell -> continuity
```
1. **Advertise** the attraction offer (often advertise the bonus/prize/free thing,
   not the core product).
2. **Attraction** converts the stranger and covers acquisition cost.
3. **Upsell** immediately after the yes — solve the next problem the purchase reveals.
4. **Downsell** anyone who declines the upsell — change how they pay or what they get.
5. **Continuity** last — never as the front-door offer (see continuity-offers.md), then
   optionally a bulk-prepaid block that auto-rolls to month-to-month.

## Mini-diagnostic — which type is missing?
- No offer that covers acquisition cost / can't afford ads -> missing **Attraction**.
- One-time sale only, nothing offered after the yes -> missing **Upsell**.
- People say no and you have nothing else to offer them -> missing **Downsell**.
- No recurring revenue / customers buy once and vanish -> missing **Continuity**.

Rule 7 still holds: **any offer, any order.** The sequence above is the default, not a law.
