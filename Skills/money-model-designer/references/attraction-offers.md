# Attraction Offers — turn strangers into customers (Stage I)

**Principle:** "free," "discount," and "$1" sit on one continuum — every one is a
discount to some level. You can make money even on "free" by requiring a purchase to
unlock more. Obey the law around the word "free" (governing rule 5).

The 5 attraction offers:

## 1. Win Your Money Back
Customer puts money down; they get it back if they do the work, get the result, or both.
- **Modes:** Results / Actions / Actions AND Results.
- **Return form:** give it back as **store credit**, not cash (rollover, below).
- **Good-offer criteria (all 3):** 1) Easy to Track, 2) Gets Customers Results,
  3) Advertises the Business.
- **Rollover store-credit example:** $200/mo product, customer wins $600 credit ->
  apply **$50/mo over 12 months** (they pay $150/mo), **not** 3 free months.
- **Decision rules:** run only if **refund rate < 5%**. Expect ~**10%** to ask for money
  back. Offer store credit instead of cash.

## 2. Giveaways (6 steps)
1. **Pick the Grand Prize** — the thing you want everyone to buy; assign a $ value anchor.
2. **Pick the Promotional Offer** — a "partial scholarship" for everyone who doesn't win.
3. **Ask for contact info + eligibility.**
4. **Pick qualifying actions** (what entrants must do to enter).
5. **Set a deadline** — 3-7 days; post daily updates.
6. **Announce the winner, then sell everyone else the discount** on a second 7-day deadline.
- **Discount rule:** the core-offer discount = **10-30% of gross margins**.
- **Diagnostic:** "If your Giveaway doesn't work, your Grand Prize wasn't grand enough."
- Give away **TWO** prizes for referral entries. Put **urgency in 3 places**: to enter,
  to claim, to use.

## 3. Decoy Offer
1. Advertise a **lesser version** as the decoy. 2. Offer both, **emphasize the premium.**
- **Build the decoy** by: fewer components, older models, removing guarantees.
- Advertise **benefits, not features**.
- **4 ways to state a discount** (on $100/mo -> $900/yr):
  1) **% off** ("25% off"), 2) **absolute** ("$300 off"), 3) **free portion**
  ("3 Months Free"), 4) **total package** ("One Year $900 ($1,200)").
- Script: *"Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?"* Discount offers have
  **higher show-up** than free ones.

## 4. Buy X Get Y Free
Sell one unit at a **higher** price and give the others **free** — do NOT discount each.
- "3 shirts for $10 each" becomes "**one shirt for $30, get two free.**"
- **Duration framing** (18 months, all cost $1,800):
  - Good: **Buy 12 Get 6 Free**
  - Better: **Buy 9 Get 9 Free**
  - Best: **Buy 6 Get 12 Free** (most free)
- **Rules:** raise prices **before** giving away; give **more free than paid**; free
  out-pulls discount; run only if you can manage the cash; **cap at 10%** of recurring
  customers.

## 5. Pay Less Now or Pay More Later
- **Pay Later:** $0 today, card on file, billed **full price** after the period unless
  they cancel — a conditional satisfaction guarantee.
- **Pay Now:** offered *after* they accept pay-later — a **20-50% discount + bonuses**
  if they pay today. Have an upsell ready.
- Promise a **clear, measurable yes/no result**.
- **Diagnostic:** if **>10%** of pay-later customers cancel, you promised too much, set
  conditions too low, or priced too high.

## Choosing among the 5
- Need trackable results + strong product -> **Win Your Money Back**.
- Big audience, want virality + a value anchor -> **Giveaway**.
- Want to make a premium look obvious -> **Decoy**.
- Physical/consumable or long commitment -> **Buy X Get Y Free**.
- Want maximum front-door conversion + a guarantee feel -> **Pay Less Now / Pay More Later**.
