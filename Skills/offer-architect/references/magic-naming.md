# Naming — the M-A-G-I-C Formula

A great offer still needs a great **name** to get the click. The name is the wrapper that makes people want to find out more. Aim for **brevity + specificity**. Use **3 to 5** of the five components — not all five, and never fewer than three.

## M-A-G-I-C

| Letter | Component | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Magnetic Reason Why** | A believable reason for the offer/attention. | Free, 88% Off, Giveaway, Spring, Grand Opening |
| **A** | **Announce the Avatar** | Call out exactly who it's for. Hyper-local beats city. | Bee Cave Dentists, Rolling Hills Moms |
| **G** | **Give a Goal** | State the dream outcome. | Pain Free, Double Your Profit, First Client |
| **I** | **Indicate a Time Interval** | Set the timeframe to the result. | 4 Hour, 21 Day, 6 Week |
| **C** | **Container Word** | The vessel that holds the offer. | Challenge, Blueprint, Bootcamp, Intensive, Incubator, Masterclass, Accelerator, Sprint, System, Transformation |

**Enhancers:** add **rhyme** or **alliteration** to make it stick.

### Worked examples
- **"Free Six-Week Lean-By-Halloween Challenge"** — M (Free) + I (Six-Week) + G (Lean) + C (Challenge) + rhyme (Lean/Halloween).
- **"5 Clients in 5 Days Blueprint"** — G (5 Clients) + I (5 Days) + C (Blueprint).

Build the name by choosing 3–5 components, drafting several candidates, then keeping the one that is shortest and most specific. This is the artifact of playbook `04-name-offer.md`.

## Offer fatigue — the 7-step variation order

The market eventually tires of any offer; response drops. When it does, change **one thing at a time, in this order** — cheapest/least disruptive change first, monetization last:

1. **Change the creative** (images/video).
2. **Change the body copy.**
3. **Change the headline / wrapper** (the MAGIC name).
4. **Rename seasonally** (re-date the same offer to a new season).
5. **Change the duration** (the time interval).
6. **Change the enhancer** (swap scarcity/urgency/bonus/guarantee mechanics).
7. **Change the monetization / price** (last resort — it touches the core economics).

**Local markets fatigue faster** than national ones — expect to walk this order sooner and more often. Keep the untried variations as a backlog so you always have the next move ready.
